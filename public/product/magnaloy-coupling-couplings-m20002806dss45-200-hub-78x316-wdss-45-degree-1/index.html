FLEXIBLE DRIVE COUPLINGS

Technical details

These bearing is a high-strength, lightweight flexible drive coupling hub from the Model 200 series. It is a mechanical coupling hub used to connect motor and pump shafts while reducing vibration and load on the system\'s actual bearings. Material : Constructed from a heat-treated Magnesium-Titanium alloy, which is 76% lighter than cast iron and 36% lighter than aluminum. Features are : High Strength-to-Weight Ratio, Corrosion Resistance, Easy Installation, Precision Machining, Radial Lug Design, Four-Way Misalignment Handling, Electrical Insulation, Maintenance-Free, Load-Lock Feature. Applications : Hydraulic Pump Drives, Compressor Systems, Conveyor Systems, Industrial Automation, Fluid Power Industry, Marine & Offshore, Aerospace & Automotive, Heavy Machinery, Construction & Agriculture. DSS45=Double Set-Screws with 45 Degrees.
