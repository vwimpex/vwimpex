LINEAR GUIDES TYPE FDB-R PAIR OF ROLLER SHOES & SINGLE RAILS LOW COST

Technical details

Franke FDB linear guides are suitable for linear motion tasks in almost all industries. They are particularly convincing in cost-sensitive applications with reduced demands on load and noise. Rail elements can be manufactured in a length of 200 mm to 4000 mm and, on request, can also be linked endlessly. Material : Cassette plate, roller shoes, Rail body : aluminum, anodized, Raceways : spring steel, Rollers : bearing steel. Lubricant : Lifetime-lubricated, maintenance-free. Operating temperature -20 °C to +80 °C.
