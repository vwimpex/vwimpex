BL SERIES LOCKNUT (BEARLOK) - INCH

Technical details

Socket Head Cap Screw = M4, THREAD (0.781-32) BL SERIES has high axial loading capacity, Self-locking, Balanced design for high speed applications. Prevents assemblies from loosening due to vibration, shock and torque uniform 360 degree thread clamping to center of shaft. Easier and faster to install than traditional locknut and lockwasher, Eliminates the need for cutting or repairing shaft keyway, Infinite adjustment and preloading capability. Does not damage shaft threads when secured, Managnese phosphate coated for corrosion resistance. Materials: Base Threaded Nut - Carbon Steel, Compression Ring - Hardened Alloy, Fasteners - Hardened Alloy. Applications: High-speed balanced & precision assemblies, Off-road equipment and machinery, Mining and mineral extraction equipment, Pulp, paper and steel mill rolls, Pumps. Max Operating Temperature : 300 Deg F.
