Technical details

These bearing is a precision-engineered elastic coupling specifically configured for use with Taper Lock Bushings. The \"TBI\" designation distinguishes it from the standard \"TB\" model by specifying that the taper bush is installed from the Inside the side facing the coupling gap. This configuration is ideal for applications where external clearance for tools is limited. The taper bush provides a secure, shrink-fit-like connection to the shaft, which eliminates the risk of \"fretting\" corrosion common in standard keyed hubs. Features are : Internal Mounting, Shaft Locking, Easy Alignment, Zero Lubrication, Zero-Displacement Insert Replacement, High Temperature Options, Long Service Life, Vibration Damping, Hub Materials, Chemical Resistance. Applications : Textile Machinery, Packaging Lines, Compressors & Blowers, Woodworking Machinery, Viscous Fluid Transfer, Food & Chemical Industries, Printing & Paper Converting, Material Handling, Power & Energy, Solar Tracking Systems, Marine & Offshore.
