FLEXIBLE DRIVE COUPLINGS

Technical details

These bearing is a high-performance Model 300 series flexible drive coupling hub specifically engineered to protect shafts and bearings by using lightweight magnesium alloy. By reducing the rotating mass of the coupling, this hub minimizes the radial and axial loads placed on the bearings of hydraulic pumps and electric motors, significantly extending their service life. Material : Magnesium alloy, which is approximately 76% lighter than cast iron and 36% lighter than aluminum. Features are : Bearing Protection, Vibration Reduction, Precision Alignment, Drive Lug Design, Electrical Insulation, Four-Way Flexibility, Lubrication-Free Operation, Noise and Vibration Damping, Quick Insert Replacement, Load-Lock Design. Applications : Conveyor Systems, Aerospace and Defense, Marine and Offshore Power, HVAC and Large Fan Drives, Industrial Compressors, Fluid Power, Construction Machinery, Agriculture Machinery.
