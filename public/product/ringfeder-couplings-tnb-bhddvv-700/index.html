ELASTOMER JAW COUPLINGS TNB

Technical details

Symmetrical design for short shaft distances, a combination of two two-part design coupling hubs and VkR buffer. The RINGFEDER TNB BHDDVV - coupling is a torsionally flexible, puncture proof claw coupling. It compensates for angular, radial and axial shaft misalignments within defined limits. The coupling transmits torque through elastic buffers loaded in shear. These buffers come in synthetic rubber (Pb) or polyurethane (Vk), as a standard VkR. These elastic buffers dampen shocks and torsional vibrations and are resistant to oil. The rotationally resilient, failsafe claw coupling RINGFEDER TNB series is flexible in all directions and therefore compensates for angular, parallel and axial shaft misalignments of the connected machines. Features are : Torsionally flexible, compensate for angular, radial and axial shaft misalignment, Buffers loaded in compression, Buffers can be replaced radially without having to move the coupled machines, Elastomers available in various shore hardness degrees, temperature ranges of –30° to +100°C.
