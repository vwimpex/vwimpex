GUIDE TRACK ROLLER BEARING

Technical details

The Nadella RKUR series is a heavy-duty eccentric guide roller with a tapered roller bearing, designed for high-precision linear guidance in industrial machinery. Its design allows for the precise adjustment of the roller\'s position, ensuring a tight fit and eliminating play within the guide system. It features a tapered roller bearing construction, allowing it to handle both high radial and axial loads. Material : The bearing is constructed from standard chrome steel for durability and robust performance. Features are : High load capacity, High speed and acceleration, Vibration and noise, Low Maintenance, Long service life. Applications : Material Handling Systems, Machine Tools, Automation and Robotics, Packaging and Printing Machines, Construction and mining equipment, Food and beverage industry, Woodworking and furniture manufacturing, Medical and electro-medical machines. R : Indicates that the guide roller is of the eccentric type.
