EXCLUDER SEALS FOR TAPERED ROLLER BEARINGS

Technical details

This Seal to be Used with (CONE) JHM840449 & (CUP) JHM840410. The CE type seal has a rubber covered 16-gauge steel case for maxjmum protection against corrosion. It also has a nose gasket that seals against the cup front face. The lip design excludes heavy contaminants, extends bearing life and allows spent grease to be flushed from the bearing cavity. CE seals are used in corrosive environments such as cargo handling equipment, drilling rigs, mine-car wheels, srupboard applications, hospital equipment, recreation equipment and utility trailers.
