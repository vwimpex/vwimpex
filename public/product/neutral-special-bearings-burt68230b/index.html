Back-Up Rollers For Metal Flattening Machines

Technical details

The bearings have a grease lubrication system and are supplied already pre-lubricated. The lubrication system foresees both the entrance and the exit of grease. The sealing system is very efficient, it does not allow outer agents (such as, dust, mill scale, humidity) to enter the back-up roll; at the same time, it prevents the leakage of grease.
