ONE-WAY CLUTCH

Technical details

The GX50 Roller Clutch is a precision-engineered one-way clutch designed to transmit torque in one direction while overrunning or freewheeling in the opposite direction. It employs high-strength rollers that engage automatically with the raceway to lock rotation, ensuring reliable performance in indexing, backstopping, or overrunning applications.
Key Features

Compact Design: Slim profile for easy integration into machinery.


High Torque Transmission: Optimized roller geometry provides maximum surface contact and high torque capacity.


Durable Construction: Made of high-grade alloy steel, heat-treated for wear resistance and long service life.


Low Maintenance: Requires minimal lubrication, reducing downtime.


Precise Engagement: Rollers lock instantly under load without slippage.


Bidirectional Options: Designed for one-way rotation, available in both clockwise and counterclockwise models.

Typical Applications

Backstopping in conveyors, gearboxes, and pumps.


Indexing drives in packaging and printing machinery.


Overrunning clutches in speed reducers, agricultural machinery, and material handling equipment.


Safety mechanisms to prevent reverse rotation.
