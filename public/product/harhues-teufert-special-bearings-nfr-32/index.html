TRACK ROLLER BEARINGS

Technical details

Thread = M 10 x 1.25. The NFR seriesis a centric guide roller manufactured by Harhues & Teufert. It is designed for a single or double-row needle roller bearing housed within a thick-walled outer ring. This construction allows it to handle both high radial forces and axial forces in either direction. These components are used in industrial applications, such as textile and material handling machinery. Material : It is made from standard bearing steel. Features are : Smooth and precise movement, Heat-stabilized, Simple installation, High load capacity, Durability and extended service life, Self-retained unit. Application : Material handling, Textile machinery, Packaging machinery, Automated machinery and robotics, Machine tools and presses, Mining machinery, Woodworking industry.
