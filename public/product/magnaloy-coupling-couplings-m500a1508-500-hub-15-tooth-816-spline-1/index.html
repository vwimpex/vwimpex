FLEXIBLE DRIVE COUPLINGS

Technical details

These bearing is a 500 Series flexible coupling hub specifically engineered for splined shaft applications. It is constructed from a high-strength magnesium-titanium alloy, making it significantly lighter than traditional cast iron or aluminum alternatives while offering superior strength and corrosion resistance. The hub is naturally rustproof and resistant to corrosion. Material : Heat-treated Magnesium Titanium alloy (76% lighter than cast iron; 36% lighter than aluminum). Features are : True Compressive Loading, High Vibration Damping, Radial Lug Orientation, High Tolerance Machining, Maintenance-Free Operation, Electrical Insulation, Shaft Engagement, Four-Way Flexibility, Load-Lock Design. Applications : Hydraulic Systems, Conveyor Systems, Industrial Pumps and Compressors, Energy & Power Generation, Food & Chemical Processing, Construction & Forestry, Agriculture Equipment.
