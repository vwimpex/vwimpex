GUIDE TRACK ROLLER BEARING

Technical details

The Nadella RKULR series is an eccentric floating guide roller with a tapered roller bearing, specifically designed for high-load and demanding linear guidance systems. It is part of Nadella\'s Heavy Line System, engineered for use in harsh industrial conditions. Material : The rolling elements and raceways are typically made from high-quality chrome steel, which is heat-treated to ensure a long service life and high load capacity. Features are : High Load Capacity, Maintenance-Free Operation, Durability and Robustness, High precision, Long service life, Reliable performance, High Speed and Acceleration, Low Noise and Vibration. Applications : Heavy-duty material handling equipment and elevators, Foundry industry machinery, Robotic and automation systems, Machine tools and general mechanical engineering, Packaging machines, Woodworking Machines. L : The bearing is a Floating guide roller, R : Right-hand eccentric version.
