DRAWN CUP ROLLER BEARING WITH OPEN END

Technical details

The Nadella DB bearing is a specific model of drawn cup needle roller bearing, notable for its compact, full-complement design which maximizes load capacity in applications where radial space is limited. This bearing consists of a thin, heat-treated steel drawn outer cup that encloses a full complement of needle rollers (without a cage). The needles are retained by their ends or by grease, and this design choice allows for more rolling elements than a caged design, directly contributing to its high radial load capacity. Material: Made from high-quality steel (bearing steel) for durability and resistance to wear and tear. Features are : High Load Handling, Space-Saving, Reliable Performance, Lightweight, Ease of Mounting, Cost-Effectiveness, Minimal Friction. Applications : Transmissions and Gearboxes, Pumps and Compressors, Presses and Conveyors, Textile Machinery, Agricultural Equipment, Packaging Machinery, Power Tools. E=Seal Incorporated At One Side Of The Bearing.
