HIGH TORQUE PLANETARY GEARBOXES

Technical details

The RE63/9 MV14X30 F07 is a high-torque planetary gearbox manufactured by Siboni, designed for demanding industrial applications.
Construction Features:
Crown Wheel: Manufactured from heat-treated steel for enhanced performance and longevity.
Gears: Mounted on roller bearings (Class 6 DIN 3962) to ensure high rigidity.
Materials: Utilizes case-hardened and tempered alloy steel for high load transmission and shock resistance.
Shafts: Made from alloy steel with high dimensional precision for optimal coupling and torque transmission.
Bearings: High-quality bearings designed for longevity under high loads.
Flanges: Output flange constructed from pressed steel with surface coating.
Input Couplings: A wide range of input couplings available for various industrial flanges; customizations upon request.
TYPICAL APPLICATIONS
Automation, robotics, and motion control systems.
Machine tools, packaging machinery.
Woodworking, textile, printing machinery.
Healthcare, pharma‑equipment.
Electric traction, conveyors, handling systems.
Anywhere combination of motor + gearbox is needed for torque boost &amp; speed reduction.
