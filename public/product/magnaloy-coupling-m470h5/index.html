Technical details

Magnaloy Coupling&rsquo;s full range of flexible inserts permit custom design performance for a wide range of applications. All insert materials offer complete electrical insulation, as Magnaloy&rsquo;s design eliminates all metal-to-metal contact.
Install the insert in the coupling positioning hubs in contact with lip around outside of insert.
Magnaloy&rsquo;s insert reduces noise and vibration and permits needed flexibility for proper operation within alignment capabilities.
Magnaloy&rsquo;s insert design will accept misalignment strain and, when excessive, will cause insert wear as a visual and audible indication of misalignment problems.
