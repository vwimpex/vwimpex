PULLEY ROLLER BEARINGS

Technical details

These bearing specialized automotive timing belt tensioner roller/pulley bearing manufactured by INA. These internal codes are assigned exclusively to custom, application-specific bearings tailored directly for Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) automotive engines. It is an integrated double-row rolling bearing structurally optimized to serve as the spinning heart inside a timing belt or auxiliary drive belt tensioner pulley assembly. Material : Manufactured from premium martensitic through-hardened alloy steel with high-temperature synthetic grease and dual rubber (NBR) seals. Features are : High Radial-Load Rigidity, Ultra-Compact Cross Section, Thermal and Environmental Resilience, Low-Friction Efficiency, Vibration and Flutter Dampening, Superfinished Rolling Tracks, High Limiting Speed Capacity, Logarithmic Roller Profile, High Shear-Stability Lubrication. Applications : Light Commercial Vehicles (LCV) & Vans, Agricultural Machinery & Compact TractorsIndustrial Stationary Engines & Power Generation.
