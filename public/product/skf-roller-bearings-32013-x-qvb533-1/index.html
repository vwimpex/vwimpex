SINGLE ROW TAPERED ROLLER BEARINGS

Technical details

SKF Single Row Tapered Roller Bearing with specific features. Tapered roller bearings are designed to handle combined radial and axial loads and offer low friction during operation. The bearing features a cup and cone design, with a separable outer ring (cup) and an inner ring assembly (cone) containing the rollers and cage. This construction facilitates easier mounting, dismounting, and maintenance. Material : The bearing is made from high-quality steel. Features are : High load capacity, Low friction, Separable components, Custom variant, Optimized surfaces, Contamination resistance, High preload capability, Consistent roller quality, Enhanced operational reliability. Applications : Wheel hubs, Gearboxes and transmissions, Rolling mills, Mining equipment, Hoisting equipment, Wind turbines, Agricultural equipment, Pumps and compressors, Conveyor systems. QVB : This is a special, non-standard.
