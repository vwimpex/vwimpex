LOCKING ELEMENTS

Technical details

Most Cost-Effective Solution of a Backlash-Free, Frcitional Shaft-Hub Con­nec­tion. High-quality Locking Elements of the series RINGFEDER RfN 8006 are used for friction-locked shaft-hub connections. They are completely wear- and maintenance-free, extremely compact and flexible to deploy, easy to assemble and disassemble and guarantee users maximum reliability and precision. Features are : Customised application, Transmission of high loads, Maximum reliability, Easy adjustability, Backlash-free, High fatigue strength under alternating torsional stresses, Wear and maintenance-free. Main Areas of Use : General Mechanical Engineering, Machine Tools, Conveying Equipment, Belt Drives, Wire and Chain Drives, Gearboxes, Pulley and Flywheel Mounting, Hammers and Presses, Pumps, Compressors, Ventilation and Fan Systems, Brake Disc Mounting, Automotive, Aerospace and Ship Construction, Excavator Construction, Mining.
