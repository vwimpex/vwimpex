SINGLE ROW TAPERED ROLLER BEARINGS

Technical details

Single row tapered roller bearings are designed to accommodate combined radial and axial loads and provide low friction during operation. The inner ring, with rollers and cage, can be mounted separately from the outer ring. These separable and interchangeable components facilitate mounting, dismounting and maintenance. By mounting one single row tapered roller bearing against another and applying a preload, a rigid bearing application can be achieved. Features are : High radial and axial load carrying capacity, High radial and axial load carrying capacity, Low friction and long service life, Separable and interchangeable components. Applications : Wheel hubs, Transmissions and gearboxes, Construction equipment, Agricultural machinery, Mining equipment, Conveyor systems, Electric motors. J2 : Pressed Steel Window-Type Cage, Q : Optimized Internal Geometry And Surface Finish.
