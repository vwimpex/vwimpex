FLEXIBLE DRIVE COUPLINGS

Technical details

These bearing is a 400 Series flexible drive coupling hub designed for precision power transmission. It is engineered to connect two shafts-typically between a motor and a pump or gearbox-while mitigating the mechanical stresses that cause premature bearing and seal failure. Material : Lightweight heat-treated magnesium alloy, which is approximately 76% lighter than cast iron and 36% lighter than aluminum while maintaining higher strength. Features are : Vibration and Noise Dampening, Precision Machining, Four-Way Flexibility, Simple Installation, Electrical Insulation, Comprehensive Misalignment Handling, Maintenance-Free Operation, High Strength-to-Weight Ratio, True Radial Lug Orientation. Applications : Hydraulic Pump Systems, Compressors and Fans, Conveyor Systems, HVAC and Plumbing, Manufacturing and Robotics, Chemical and Oil & Gas, Agricultural Equipment, Material Handling, Manufacturing and Robotics, Marine and Offshore, Renewable Energy. DSS45=Double Set-Screws with 45 Degrees.
