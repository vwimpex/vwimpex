SIT LOCK CAL1 (NOT SELF-CENTERING)

Technical details

Cone clamping unit consists of four pieces with two inside double-cone rings joined through a set of tightening screws. It is recommended for medium to high torques and although it is not self-centering, it can be easily assembled and disassembled. Available for shaft diameters from 20 to 900 mm. Medium-high torque, Wide tolerances, Easy dismantling.
