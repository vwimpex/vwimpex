Technical details

An RSLI 59 RU-Steel coupling is a specific model of flexible, full-steel transmission coupling manufactured by RU-STEEL, designed for high-torque industrial applications requiring flexibility with torsional rigidity. It features a lamellar pack design with flexible elements in stainless steel to connect hubs and a spacer for a balanced, high-efficiency power transmission. This coupling is ATEX certified for use in explosive environments and is engineered to withstand shock and vibration while offering durable, long-lasting performance.
RSLI 59 is a model name within RU-STEEL&#39;s range of elastic and flexible transmission couplings. It is a &quot;full steel&quot; coupling, meaning its construction is entirely metallic. The flexible elements are made from stainless steel (e.g., AISI 304), providing flexibility and torsional rigidity.
