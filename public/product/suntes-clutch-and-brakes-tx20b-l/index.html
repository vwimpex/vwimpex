Technical details

The SUNTES TX..B-L is an automatic-reset, ball-clutch type mechanical torque limiter / torque releaser engineered by the Japanese industrial manufacturer SANYO SHOJI CO., LTD. It provides highly reliable, instantaneous overload protection for power transmission shafts. When driven system resistance spikes beyond a pre-calibrated limit, the device mechanically cuts power transmission to shield motors, gearboxes, and downstream machinery from catastrophic failure. The product line architecture. It uses a continuous/automatic reset ball-detent mechanism that resets mechanically every 360° rotation once load drops. Features are : High-Speed Rating, Axial Displacement Signal, Low-Inertia, Compact Layout, Low-Stiffness Coil Setup, Wide, Linear Torque Adjustability. Applications : Automated Assembly & Electronics Manufacturing, Packaging & Labeling Machinery, Food, Beverage & Pharmaceutical Processing, Printing & Textile Automation, Machine Tool & Laboratory Equipment. B=Sprocket Type Integration, L=The Low-Torque Spring Rating (White Springs).
