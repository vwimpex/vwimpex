SELF ALIGNING BALL BEARING

Technical details

Self-aligning ball bearings have two rows of balls, a common sphered raceway in the outer ring and two deep uninterrupted raceway grooves in the inner ring. They are insensitive to angular misalignment of the shaft relative to the housing, which can be caused, for example, by shaft deflection. Features are : Accommodate static and dynamic misalignment, Excellent high-speed performance, Excellent light load performance, Low friction, Maintenance-free, Contamination resistance, Reduced friction and heat, Consistent lubrication, Lightweight, Optimized internal geometry, Extended service life. Applications : Food and beverage industry, Agricultural machinery, Material handling, Industrial fans, Pulp and paper industry, Pumps and blowers, Transmission shafts, Industrial machinery. E : Optimized Internal Design, TN9 : Injection Moulded Snap-Type Cage Of Glass Fibre Reinforced Polyamide 6,6, Ball Centered, W64 : Solid Oil Filling.
