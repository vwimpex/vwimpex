FLEXIBLE DRIVE COUPLINGS

Technical details

These bearing is a Magnaloy 400 Series flexible drive coupling hub. It is specifically designed as a splined bore hub made from a lightweight magnesium-titanium alloy, making it significantly lighter than traditional cast iron or aluminum alternatives while maintaining high strength. Features are : Reduced Bearing and Component Load, Precision Vibration Dampening, True Radial Lug Design, Full Electrical Insulation, Four-Way Misalignment Compensation, Rustproof and Corrosion-Resistant, Tool-Free Alignment, High Strength-to-Weight Ratio, Maintenance-Free Operation. Applications : Conveyor Systems, Power Transmission, Manufacturing & Automotive, Hydraulic Pump Drives, Agriculture & Construction, Food & Beverage, Mining and Steel Production, Compressors and Blowers. C=Clamp Feature.
