Details

Mounted Cast Iron Two Bolt Pillow Block Ball Bearing - 52100 Bearing Steel, Black Oxided - Setscrew Lock

Technical details

NP-22 = Complete Bearing with Housing, 2-16 = Bearing Insert. (NP Series Standard Duty Pillow Blocks - Setscrew Locking). Powder coated epoxy cast iron housing with extended inner ring bearings. Zone hardened inner ring with 120° spacing setscrew locking with diamond faceted setscrews. Unique land riding retainer design provides superior pocket clearance that allows for 360° grease circulation around the rolling elements resulting in better retained and utilized lubrication. Lock pin and dimple for direct lubrication and anti-rotation.
