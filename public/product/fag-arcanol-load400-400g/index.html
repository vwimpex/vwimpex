Technical details

ARCANOL-LOAD400-400G [FAG] [C62/GRM/400]
The rolling bearing greases Arcanol offer very good conditions for a favorable running behavior of the bearings as well as for a long service life and high operational reliability of the bearing. The lubricant range is graded so that it covers almost all areas of application. With modern test behavior and test systems, the area of ​​application of Arcanol greases was determined under different operating conditions and with roller bearings of all types. Arcanol rolling bearing greases have better properties than normal greases in all areas.
