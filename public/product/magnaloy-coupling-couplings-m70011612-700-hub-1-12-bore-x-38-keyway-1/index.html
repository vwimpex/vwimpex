FLEXIBLE DRIVE COUPLINGS

Technical details

These bearing is a 700 Series flexible drive coupling hub, distinguished as part of the original lightweight, heavy-duty line of couplings designed for high-torque industrial applications. It is manufactured from a specialized Magnesium Titanium alloy, which provides a strength-to-weight ratio superior to both cast iron and aluminum. Material : Magnesium Titanium alloy (76% lighter than cast iron; 36% lighter than aluminum). Features are : Tight Machining Tolerances, Vibration & Noise Reduction, Four-Way Flexibility, Radial Lug Design, Reduced Bearing Loads, Electrical Insulation, No Lubrication Required, Quick Insert Replacement, Shaft Gap Accommodation. Applications : Fluid Power Systems, Gearboxes and Turbines, Construction & Agricultural Machinery, Medical & Laboratory Equipment, Material Handling & Conveyor System, Packaging Machinery, Power Plants & Water Facilities.
