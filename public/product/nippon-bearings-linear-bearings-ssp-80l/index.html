CYLINDRICAL SPLINE NUT (STANDARD TYPE)

Technical details

Nippon Bearing (NB) ball spline, the SSP series. It is a cylindrical type spline nut designed for linear motion applications, offering smooth and precise movement due to the rolling action of ball elements within a nut and shaft assembly. Features are : Smooth Motion, High Load Capacity, Precise Positioning, High-Speed Performance, Compact Design. Ball splines are used in various applications, including CNC machines, robotics, and other automation equipment. The \"SSP\" indicates the series (Cylindrical Ball Spline),
