Technical details

ELASTOMER COUPLING, EKL SERIES, COMPACT VERSION WITH CLAMPING HUB, SIZE 300, 84NM RATED TORQUE, 82MM LENGTH, PLEASE SPECIFY D1 AND D2 (BORE DIAMETERS, RANGING FROM 20MM TO 45MM). The EKL R+W Coupling is a backlash-free, vibration-damping compact jaw coupling. It is engineered with a shorter overall length than the standard EK series, making it ideal for high-precision servo applications where installation space is restricted. The Elastomer Insert type. Type \"C\" typically refers to a Shore hardness of 80 Sh A, which is the softest standard option, providing high vibration damping. Material : High-strength aluminum hubs; wear-resistant TPU elastomer. Features are : Compact Design, Clamping Hub System, Electrical Isolation, Misalignment Compensation, Maintenance-Free, Infinite Service Life, High-Speed Stability, Precision Tolerances, Space-Saving Design, Temperature Resilience. Applications : Servo Drive Technology, Machine Tools, Packaging Machinery, Automation & Robotics, Printing & Textile Machinery, Medical & Laboratory Equipment, Pumps and Agitators, Woodworking Machinery, Metalworking & Gear Cutting, Conveyor and Crane Systems.
