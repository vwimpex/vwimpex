TRACK ROLLER BEARINGS

Technical details

Threadd = M 6 x 1.0. The FR series guide roller is a single-row track roller bearing manufactured by Harhues & Teufert GmbH for use in linear guidance systems. Its robust design allows it to handle high radial and axial loads. The FR series is a self-retained bearing, featuring a thick-walled outer ring. It is mounted on a threaded stud for easy installation. Material : It is manufactured from high-quality bearing steel, providing excellent durability and strength for industrial applications. Features are : High load capacity, Corrosion resistance, Reduced friction, Longevity, Mounting options, Sealed for protection, High precision, Maintenance-free. Applications : Conveyor systems, Automated machinery and robotics, Material handling equipment, Packaging machines, Machine tools and presses, Automotive industry, Aerospace and defense, Textile machinery, Printing presses.
