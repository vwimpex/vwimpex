CARRIAGE BEARING HOUSINGS

Technical details

TVN Plummer block bearing housings are designed for use with cylidrical bore, self aligning ball bearings. These housings are unsplit and are supplied as floating bearings. A locating bearing arrangement is achieved by inserting fixing rings or intermediate rings. Sealing is by means of felt strip. TVN housings are made from GG20. For lubrication of these bearing units it is recommended that a high grade grease be used. A R1/8\" grease hole is incorporated in the housing for eventual later greasing.
