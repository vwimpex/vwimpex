METAL BELLOWS COUPLING

Technical details

METAL BELLOWS COUPLING, BK1 SERIES, FLANGE MOUNTING, 30NM RATED TORQUE, 44MM LENGTH, STAINLESS STEEL BELLOWS. These bearing is a specific model within the R+W BK1 series of metal bellows couplings, designed for high-precision industrial applications requiring flange mounting. It is engineered for applications requiring absolute zero backlash and extreme torsional rigidity, such as high-performance servo axles and CNC machinery. Material : The bellows are made from high-grade flexible stainless steel, while the hubs/flanges are constructed from steel. Features are : Misalignment Compensation, Maintenance-Free, Compact Flange Mounting, Infinite Service Life, Low Moment of Inertia, High Dynamic Balancing, Overload Protection, Temperature Stability. Applications : Robotics & Automation, Packaging & Labeling, Printing & Textile Machinery, Medical Technology, Test Benches & Rigs, Woodworking Machinery, Sheet Metal Processing, Optical & Measuring Instruments, Potentiometers & Tachometers.
