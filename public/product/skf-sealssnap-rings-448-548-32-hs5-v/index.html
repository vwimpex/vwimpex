Radial Shaft Seal For Heavy Industrial Applications, Fluoro Rubber (FKM)

Technical details

HS5 seals are designed for housings with a cover plate. The SKF Springcover provides reliable spring retention for a blind seal installation. These seals perform exceptionally well in very large-size machinery, and customized or heavy-duty machinery. They are designed for industries such as metal, construction, wind energy, forestry, mining, and pulp and paper.
