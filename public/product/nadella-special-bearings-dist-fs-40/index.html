Technical details

The DIST FS series refers to a specific Nadella linear guide rail (part of the FS series). The components for this system, such as bearings, are typically sold separately or as part of a complete linear guide system. These are designed to run along the guide rail. Nadella produces various types, including those with full complement needle rollers for high load capacity and rigidity. These are used in applications requiring high load capacity within limited space. Used in linear guide systems, often in combination with guide rollers, to create circular, oval, or ring-shaped motion circuits. Material : The standard rails are made of steel. The raceways (running surfaces) are induction hardened to ensure durability and handle high loads.The rails have the same section dimensions as straight FS M rails and are joined using alignment blocks to ensure precise assembly. Applications : Robotics & Automation, Packaging & Processing, Machine Tools, Material Handling, Woodworking & Plastics, Medical & Precision Instruments, Printing & Paper Processing, Aerospace Components.
