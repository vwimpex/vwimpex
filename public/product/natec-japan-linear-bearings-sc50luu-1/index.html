SLIDE UNIT SC TYPE

Technical details

SC Series Linear Bushing (Slide Unit). The linear bushing supports and guides a load, enabling smooth and accurate linear motion along a specified path. Material: Aluminum housing with integrated ball bearings, outer casing of Chromium Steel. Features are : Smooth and Low-Friction Movement, High Rigidity and Long Life, Ease of Maintenance, Long Type with Wide Block Housing, Dust-Proof Seal, Durability and Longevity, Easy Installation. Applications: Commonly used in 3D printers, CNC (Computer Numerical Control) machines, home appliances, industrial settings, low-noise and high-speed electric motors, construction machinery, automotive components, pumps, and other machinery. UU : Both Sides Sealed, L : Long-type Linear Ball Bearing.
