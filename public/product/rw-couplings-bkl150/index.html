MINIATURE METAL BELLOWS COUPLING

Technical details

MINIATURE METAL BELLOWS COUPLING, BKL/150 SERIES, ECOFLEX WITH CLAMPING HUB, SIZE150, 150NM RATED TORQUE, PLEASE SPECIFY D1 AND D2 (BORE DIAMETERS, RANGING FROM 19MM TO 42MM), STAINLESS STEEL BELLOWS. These bearing is a precision metal bellows coupling from the \"Economy\" series, designed for backlash-free torque transmission in high-dynamic servomotor and automation applications. It combines lightweight aluminum hubs with high-grade stainless steel bellows to provide high torsional rigidity and a low moment of inertia. Material : Bellows are made of high-grade stainless steel; hubs are typically aluminum but optionally available in steel. Features are : Easy Mounting System, Misalignment Compensation, Maintenance-Free, Wear-Free Construction, Wide Temperature Range, Overload Protection, High-Speed Stability, Infinite Service Life, ATEX Compliance. Applications : CNC Machinery, Industrial Robotics, Medical Equipment, Packaging & Printing Machinery, Textile & Woodworking Machines, Assembly & Processing Plants, Electronics & Semiconductor Manufacturing, Food & Beverage Processing, Renewable Energy.
