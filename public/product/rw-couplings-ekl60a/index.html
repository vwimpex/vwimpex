Technical details

ELASTOMER COUPLING, EKL SERIES, COMPACT VERSION WITH CLAMPING HUB, SIZE 60, 60NM RATED TORQUE, 58MM LENGTH, PLEASE SPECIFY D1 AND D2 (BORE DIAMETERS, RANGING FROM 12MM TO 32MM). The EKL is a compact, elastomer coupling manufactured by R+W Coupling Technology. It is designed for precision power transmission in confined spaces, featuring a vibration-damping elastomer insert between two high-strength aluminium clamping hubs. Elastomer Insert (A) refers to the hardness of the preloaded insert, typically a Shore 98A (Red) TPU, which offers a balance of damping and torsional stiffness. Material : Hubs are made of high-strength aluminium, and the elastomer is wear-resistant and thermally stable. Features are : Electrical Insulation, Press-Fit Assembly, High Speed Rating, Temperature Resistance, Frictional Connection, Easy Mounting, Maintenance-Free, Misalignment Tolerance, Low Moment of Inertia. Applications : Servo Drive Technology, Medical & Laboratory Equipment, Packaging & Printing Machinery, Machine Tools & CNC, Automation & Robotics, Semiconductor Manufacturing, Aerospace & Defense, Textile Machinery.
