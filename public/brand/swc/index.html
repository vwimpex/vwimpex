SWC offers variouse products suitable for high temperature applications. In order to choose the right bearing, the temperature as well as the speeds and the load ratings have to be taken into account.

  • High-temp Deep Groove Ball Bearings

Coated bearings for extreme temperatures and slow speeds

HT1 (< 350°C) SWC High-temp Deep Groove Ball Bearings with the suffix HT1 are filled with a high-temp paste,
which hardens after reaching a certain temperature and leaves a powder.

Bearings lubricated with HT1 are suitable for slow speeds and temperatures up to 350°C,
but have to be relubricated or replaced after 12 to 18 months depending on the application.

All HT1 bearings are available open, with shield on one (ZR) or both sides (2ZR).

  • HT2 (< 280°C)

SWC High-temp Deep Groove Ball Bearings with the suffix HT2 are filled with a special grease.

Bearings lubricated with HT2 are suitable for slow speeds and temperatures up to 280°C.
Depending on the application, these bearings allow maintenance-free operation up to 5 years.

All HT2 bearings are available open, with shield on one (ZR) or both sides (2ZR).

  • HT2X (< 320°C)

SWC High-temp Deep Groove Ball Bearings with the suffix HT2X are filled with a special grease.

Bearings lubricated with HT2X are suitable for slow speeds and temperatures up to 320°C.
Depending on the application, these bearings allow maintenance-free operation up to 5 years.

All HT2X bearings are available open, with shield on one (ZR) or both sides (2ZR).

  • HT3 (< 300°C)

SWC High-temp Deep Groove Ball Bearings with the suffix HT3 are filled with a solid compound.
While running, small particals of the compound come lose and lubricate the bearing continously.

Bearings filled with HT3 are suitable for slow speeds and temperatures up to 300°C.
Depending on the application, these bearings allow maintenance-free operation up to 6 years.

All HT3 bearings are only available with shields on both sides (2ZR).
