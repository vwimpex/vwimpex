Brand

Helix Linear

Get a Helix Linear quote

Helix Linear Technologies is an ISO 9001:2015 registered company with a rich history in innovative linear motion products. Our sales and engineering teams work with our customers to develop linear motion solutions that ensure superior functionality and efficiency.

Technology drives our processes, but customer focus and dedicated team members drive our success. We are vertically integrated to ensure manufacturability, product quality, process integrity, cost efficiency, and fast delivery.

  • LEAD SCREWS AND ACME SCREWS

Helix Linear Technologies offers more than 300 different lead screw sizes and dozens of different standard nut designs including, freewheeling nuts, anti-backlash nuts and custom nuts.

We have manufactured precision lead screws and acme screws for more than 50 years. Our CNC thread rolling systems guarantee accurate positioning to 0.0003 in/in.

  • ACME SCREWS

Helix Linear Technologies Acme screws are manufactured to the standard 2C thread form. Screws can be cut to your specific length or we can machine the ends of the screws for you.

  1. • Imperial sizes from 0.250 - 1.500"
  2. • 2C Acme Thread Form
  3. • Lead Accuracy - 0.0003"/Inch
  • TWIN-LEAD SCREWS

Helix Linear twin-lead screws are designed with a left and right hand thread for dual and opposing motion. Helix offers multiple sizes of twin-lead screws as standard products and manufactures nearly any size screw on request.

  • BALL SCREW AND ACME SCREW BEARING MOUNTS

The Helix Linear Technologies bearing mounts for ball screws and Acme screws help engineers easily build machines with lead screws and ball screws.

  • LINEAR LUBRICANTS

Almost all linear motion systems require lubrication to operate precisely and reduce wear. Helix Linear Technologies offers a full line of linear motion lubricants that are specific to your application.

  • STEPPER ACTUATORS

Helix stepper motor actuators feature heavy duty ball bearings to maximize their thrust. Helix lead screws are securely press-fitted into the motor’s rotor to allow a smaller footprint, while minimizing backlash and providing years of dependable life.

  1. External
  2. Non-Captive

Optional available accessories include: connectors, wire harnesses, encoders, and custom lead nuts.

  • BALL SCREW STEPPER MOTOR ACTUATORS

NEMA 8, 11, 14, 17, 23, 34 Single and Double Stack Motors.

Today’s linear motion and automation challenges require higher precision and faster speeds. Ball screws matched with a stepper motor from Helix Linear Technologies allow engineers to design highly repeatable motion control systems that can perform at high duty-cycles.

  • CAN-STACK STEPPER MOTORS

Can-stack linear actuators offer an economical linear motion solution in a small package with linear step accuracy ranging from 3 – 400 microns, and dual ball bearings for greater precision and lifespan.

  • PROFILE RAIL LINEAR ACTUATORS

PRA Linear Actuators combine all of the primary aspects of a precise linear motion system in a small and economical package - hybrid stepper motor, precision lead screw, digital rotary encoder and a rugged profile rail for guidance. These stepper motor linear actuators are offered in NEMA 8,11,14,17and 23 motor sizes. Configure solid models online.
