Hub City is one of the world's largest manufacturers of heavy duty power transmission products. From gear drives and speed reducers to couplings and bearings, each Hub City line of products is manufactured to the highest level of quality and engineered with 120 years of industry knowledge.

Hub City offers power transmission products for applications in recreation, transportation, military, packaging, construction, communication, material handling, medical and food processing equipment.

Hub City's major product Lines are: Bearings, Electric Motors, Farm Products & Accessories, Gear Drives, Gearmotors, High Efficiency Helical Drives, Speed Reducers, Washdown Products and Custom Products.

More than half of Hub's products are either modified or completely customized. One of the greatest benefits of doing business with Hub City is the strength of its engineering capabilities. Hub City prides itself in filling its customers' needs with cost effective solutions.

  • Hub City Standard Products:

In addition to its successful custom product line, Hub City offers one an extensive, high quality standard product line, including a wide range of worm gear, bevel, parallel shaft and in-line speed reducers, mounted bearings and the Mina-Gear® subfractional horsepower gearmotors.

Recent product line additions include the following: PowerTorque® Shaft mount reducers featuring ductile iron housings and precision ground gearing. DuraLine Mounted Bearings provide long-life and economy in the most popular sizes of pillow blocks, flange blocks and flange bracket bearings.

  • CleanLine Ultraplus Stainless Steel Bearings

Hub City CleanLine Ultraplus Stainless Steel Bearings304 Stainless Steel Housings or Ultradur®. 440 Stainless Steel Rings and Balls, 302 Stainless Steel Flingers and Retainers. Stainless steel housings are machine polished to NSF/3-A or Industry Approved Standards.

These products include stainless steel and composite thermoplastic mounted bearings and stainless steel or acrylic painted gear drives. Four levels of washdown protection make up the product offering. CleanLine gear drives feature acrylic painted housings and stainless steel shafts. CleanLine Plus gear drives feature aluminum Platinum Plus housings and stainless steel shafts. CleanLine Plus mounted bearings feature composite thermoplastic housings with stainless steel inserts. The CleanLine UltraPlus mounted bearings feature stainless steel housings and inserts.

The Hub City stainless steel design featured in the CleanLine UltraPlus drives and bearings goes beyond basic corrosion resistance, by providing a smooth unobstructed, contoured exterior surface. The exterior is free of ribs, undercuts and protrusions that do not support washdown, cleaning and sanitary conditions.

CleanLine UltraPlus Hub3® (Hub Cube) Gear Drives are provided with stainless steel housings, shafts and hardware. These drives are available in seven sizes of worm gear reducers and three models of bevel gear drives. Smooth polished surfaces all around identify these drives as the ultimate food processing and/or washdown solutions.
