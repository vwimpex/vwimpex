<strong>MRC is an SKF Company.</strong></p>
<p> MRC products offer unique solutions to application challenges - hard-to-find solutions that are often unavailable from any other supplier.</p>
<p> MRC specialty bearings and housings are available right off the shelf as "drop-in" replacements, ready for work within a variety of machines everything from gearboxes, pumps and electric motors to auxiliary power units, conveyors . MRC products are helping plants across a range of industries operate more productively and profitably.</p>
<p>MRC bearings have for many years been used at the centre of countless aerospace bearing applications. MRC's aerospace products are primarily found in engine and gearbox applications for both fixed and rotary wing requirements. Most of MRC's aerospace engine and gearbpx bearing range takes the form of special designs tailored to specific application criteria.</p>
