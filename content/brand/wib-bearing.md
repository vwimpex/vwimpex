<p>WIB, a Swiss company founded in 1961, specializes in the development and production of HIGH QUALITY PRECISION BALL BEARINGS. The WIB trade mark is registered in 13 different countries, both in Europe and on the American continent. About 95% of WIB production output is exported to over 30 industrialized countries.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Standard products from WIB bearings include:</strong></li>
</ul>
<ol>
<li> Single row radial ball bearings (metric)</li>
<li> Single row track rollers (cylindrical and spherical)</li>
<li> Double row angular contact ball bearings</li>
<li> Double row track rollers (cylindrical and spherical)</li>
<li> Double row track rollers (with gothic groove)</li>
<li> Self-aligning ball bearing</li>
</ol>
<ul>
<li><strong>Special, Non-Standard Bearings from WIB</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>However, more than half of WIB production is special and custom bearings designed according to individual customer specification and based on a large offering of standard bearings. Some of the special bearings offered by WIB bearing include:</p>
<ol>
<li> Wire Guides and Straightening Rollers</li>
<li> Bearings for Linear Guide Systems</li>
<li> Bearings for Textile Machines</li>
<li> Special bearings for other applications</li>
<li> Manufacturing of high precision components</li>
