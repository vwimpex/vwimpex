<p>The <strong>MCGILL®</strong> brand has more than 100 years of bearing industry experience as the inventor of the cam follower. McGill products solve problems in a wide variety of applications.</p>
<p>McGill bearings provide solutions to a wide variety of industrial applications. The branded products include: </p>
<ul>
<li>CAMROL</li>
<li>TRAKROL</li>
<li>CAGEROL</li>
<li>GUIDEROL</li>
<li>SPHERE-ROL </li>
</ul>
<p>In 1937, McGill engineers invented the first needle bearing cam follower. Since that time, McGill has maintained its leading position through the continuous development of new features and improvements to the CAMROL bearing product line.</p>
<p>As today’s leading manufacturer of quality cam follower bearings, McGill has developed many features to extend bearing life for a variety of operating conditions, lubrication requirements and application environments. McGill offers the broadest range of cam follower bearings on the market with over 1,400 standard designs to choose from.</p>
