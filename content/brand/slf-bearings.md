<p><strong>Spindel- und Lagerungstechnik Fraureuth GmbH (SLF)</strong> since 1993. In this regard, SLF successfully carrying forward a multi-year tradition that began in 1943 in the domain of constructing and manufacturing spindle units and ball bearings.<br> SLF delivers customized solutions for demanding customers active in industries such as mechanical engineering, food & beverage, motor manufacturing or pump manufacturing. SLF is headquartered in Fraureuth, a production site with a long history of specialized spindles and ball bearings manufacturing dating back more than 60 years. SLF was founded in 1993 and is today regarded as a leading German manufacturer of specialized spindles and ball bearings.</p>
