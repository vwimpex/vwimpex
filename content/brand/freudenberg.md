<p>As the leading specialist in sealing applications and their market, Freudenberg-NOK Sealing Technologies is a supplier as well as a development and service partner serving customers in a wide variety of industries, including fluid power, automotive tier and construction.</p>
<p>stands for innovative competence, a customer orientation and technological know-how, for trustworthy reliability and the highest quality at a fair price. In-depth material expertise provides the basis for customized sealing solutions.</p>
<p>Based on the Simmerring® which was developed by Freudenberg in 1929, Freudenberg-NOK Sealing Technologies has built up a broad and continuously expanding range of seals and vibration control technology products. Together with its partners in Japan, India and China, Freudenberg-NOK Sealing Technologies has formed a global network with the aim of offering products of the same high quality.</p>
