<p><strong>URB</strong> Started Factory In 1953 At Barlad in Romania, URB Has a Long Tradition in Manufacturing & Supply of World Class Bearings For More Than 65 Years.</p>
<p>The <strong>URB®</strong> brand is produced by S.C. "RULMENTI" S.A. Barlad (Romania), a worldwide supplier of bearings and services in the rolling bearing business. The company is among the biggest producers of rolling bearings in southeastern Europe and employs about 3,100 people worldwide.</p>
<p>URB bearing products are well-known for their precision and reliability and include, among other products, the following:</p>
<ul>
<li>Cylindrical Roller Bearings</li>
<li>Spherical Roller Bearings</li>
<li>Radial Ball Bearings</li>
<li>Thrust Ball Bearings</li>
<li>Thrust Cylindrical Roller Bearings</li>
</ul>
<p>Their Bearings Have Been Successfully Running Across the Globe Meeting The Varied Application Demands Of Industrial Segments Like:</p>
<ul>
<li>Metals (Steel Making & Rerolling)</li>
<li>Mining & Mineral Processing (Crushing, Screening, Conveying)</li>
<li>Industrial Gearboxes (Heavy Duty Gearboxes)</li>
<li>Cement & Aggregates,</li>
<li>Petroleum Industry (Oil Exploration)</li>
<li>Railways (Registered with Railway System Of 6 Countries)</li>
<li>Wind Energy,</li>
<li>Agriculture,</li>
<li>Heavy Trucks & Off Highway Vehicles,</li>
<li>Elevators, Cranes & Material Handling Equipment Etc.</li>
</ul>
