<p>Started in 1946, SNR now have a presence in over 200 countries and across all continents. Employing approximately 3700 people and as part of a subsidiary of Renault SA, SNR have a strong footing when tendering on a global basis. SNR's product range includes instrument bearings, ABS wheel speed sensor bearings, high-resolution sensor line encode bearings and LubSolid Bearings.</p>
<p>SNR, a brand known for its quality automotive, industrial, and aerospace bearings, is being managed by NTN-SNR, the European arm of NTN Corp.</p>
<p>SNR bearings is a brand with a solid history of quality and reliability. Currently based in France, this notable brand was founded in the late 1800s by Jacques Schmidt. Schmidt manufactured the first rings and balls of the current tempered steel bearings. Having opened a factory in France during World War I, SNR supplied then the needs of the French military aeronautical industry and is known as the first bearing company to obtain ISO-9001 certification.</p>
<p>The SNR bearings catalog features a wide range of bearings for different fields and industries.</p>
