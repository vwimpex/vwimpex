<p>KSK Bearings is a leading manufacturer of radial ball bearings. Based in Japan, KSK excels at providing quality products for a variety of markets.</p>
<h3><strong>Radial Ball Bearings and Other Products from KSK.</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li>Currently produce for a major Japanese bearing manufacturer.</li>
<li>Production facility is located near Osaka, Japan.</li>
<li>Focus on Electric Motor bearings, Automotive bearings, & Special bearings.</li>
<li>Can manufacture product in a variety of materials including SUJ2, SUS440C, SUS420J2, and QD51.</li>
<li>Offer inch series bearings, with some metric options.</li>
<li>A variety of sealing options are available including non-contact seals.</li>
<li>OD modifications such as bearing snap rings and grooves are available.</li>
</ul>
