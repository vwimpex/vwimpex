<p><strong>Crawford Precision Engineering (CPE) </strong>is the manufacturer of the ROTOLIN and SPIRO range of Rotary Linear Bearings, Self Aligning Bearings, Ball Bushings and Ball Units in standard and special configurations. CPE have many years experience in the manufacture of bearings with customers all over the UK, Europe and the American continent. We have recently added the EXACTA range of precision die set components to our product portfolio. Our products are widely used in the paper processing industry (Doctor Back Bearings) and in many special purpose machine applications.</p>
<p>The extensive range of ROTOLIN bearings are designed for universal applications with rotary and linear motion, separately or simultaneously. The rolling balls are preloaded to ensure accuracy and eliminate shake or deflection</p>
<p><strong>LINEAR / ROTARY BEARINGS:</strong><br> Special bearings have been designed to suit specific customer needs, from Aircraft to Machine Tools, Paper Making to Nuclear Power, and can include self aligning facilities and eccentric motion as necessary. </p>
