<p>The Family-Owned and operated company is founded in Rheinau-Freistett. Zimmer GmbH quickly established itself in the field of developing and manufacturing highly complex components and system solutions for prestigious OEM customers.</p>
<p>More than 20 years of development and market experience have yielded more than 4,000 products. Zimmer Group offers the most comprehensive and innovative portfolio of products and services reflecting the highest possible quality and reliability.</p>
<p><strong>Zimmer</strong> offer high performance, maximum quality clamping and braking elements for linear positioning applications. Designed to suit most brands of linear ball guide and round shaft products, the innovative clamps are mounted beside linear carriages and pillow blocks to provide either hand, pneumatic or hydraulic clamping, as well as braking options.</p>
<p>Zimmer clamping and braking elements offer a new and innovative approach for positioning, holding and stopping applications. They ensure precision during cutting processes and boost efficiency with short cycle times. Here are some of the key benefits:</p>
<ul>
<li>Small size with incredible holding force</li>
<li>No relative movement for the workpiece</li>
<li>No active clamping force on the guide carriage</li>
<li>High positioning accuracy</li>
<li>High rigidity</li>
<li>Long lifecycle</li>
<li>Easy to install</li>
<li>Excellent value</li>
<li>Custom solutions available on request</li>
<li>Series with special friction coating for brake lining material</li>
</ul>
