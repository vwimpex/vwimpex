<ul>
<li><strong> V-Rings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>The V-ring is an all-rubber seal. It mounts directly on the shaft and seals axially against the counter-face, housing, bearing race, and similar surfaces. Designed with a long flexible lip, it acts like a mechanical face seal and a lip seal. It can retain lubricants or exclude contaminants. The V-ring also can be used as the primary seal or as a secondary back-up seal. And it is capable of handing a greater amount of eccentricity and misalignment as well as axial movement. Each V-ring can be used for a range of different shaft sizes.</p>
