<p>BRL Bearings SL is a distributor based in the Barcelona Region, we specialise in <strong>rotational solutions</strong> for high temperature applications predominantly in the bearing series of 6000, 6200 and 6300 which can handle temperatures from 150ºC to 330ºC.</p>
<p>BRL has designed boxes in different colours for each product range according to their respective working temperatures, making it easier to identify and describe them in your warehouse.</p>
<p>The <strong>ENC High Temperature Bearings</strong> are specially designed for applications that must work in high temperature conditions.</p>
<p>These bearings are made of AISI 52100 steel and incorporate as lubrication solution the most suitable greases for each thermal grade.</p>
<p>Their ENC temperature bearings are especially useful in applications working in conditions subject to high temperatures such as trolleys used in kilns for brick making, in furnaces for the ceramic industry, in rollers working in conditions of elevated temperatures, in the steel manufacturing industry , aluminum manufacturing, glass manufacturing, Steel industry, Etc.</p>
