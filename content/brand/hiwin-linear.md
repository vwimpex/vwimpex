<p>It was founded in 1989 and is based in Taichung, Taiwan. <strong>HIWIN</strong> is a combination of HI-tech Winner.</p>
<p><strong>Product Overview:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Linear Guideways</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Linear guideways provide a type of motion that utilizes re-circulating rolling elements between a profile rail and a bearing block.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Ballscrews</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Ballscrews consist of a screw spindle and a nut integrated with rolling elements that cycle through a return mechanism.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Linear Motors</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Hiwin linear motors are direct driven axis with plug and play capabilities.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Single Axis Robots</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Hiwin's single axis robots utilize a combination of a ballscrew and linear guideways or a belt-driven system that are motor driven.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Crossed Roller Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Hiwin Crossed Roller Bearings mainly consist of an outer ring, an inner ring, a plurality of rollers and a plurality of spacers.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Harmonic Gearing Systems</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>A harmonic gearing system has three basic components: a wave generator, a flex spline, and a circular spline.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Torque Motor Rotary Tables</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Torque motor and rotary tables ensure excellent acceleration capabilities and high precision uniform movements</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Positioning Measurement Systems</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Hiwin's positioning guideway system integrates a linear guideway with a magnetic encoder</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>AC Servo Motors and Drives</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Hiwin offers AC servo motors and stepper motors, which when combined with another Hiwin product line are great solutions for converting rotary motion into linear motion</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Articulated Robots</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Articulated Robots are used when complex motions, faster cycle time, and high repeatability are priorities.Learn more</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>HIWIN Grease</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Hiwin offers lubricants for each type of environment such as general type, heavy load, low particle emitting, high speed, etc.Learn more</p>
