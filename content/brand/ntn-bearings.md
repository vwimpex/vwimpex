<p>Established in 1963, NTN is one of the world's largest bearing producers. With manufacturing throughout the world, NTN is a leading bearing supplier to both the industrial and automotive markets. NTN is also a leader in the development of precision equipment in cutting-edge fields and supplies many different industries, including automotive, construction equipment, wind energy, and steel and paper mills.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>NTN Ball Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>NTN ball bearings are available in sizes ranging from 10 mm to 320 mm outside diameters and in a wide variety of configurations of seals, shields, lubricants, internal clearances, and cage designs. With more than ninety years of manufacturing expertise in ball bearings, NTN offers one of the industry’s widest arrays of products to fit your application.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>NTN Deep Groove Ball Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>NTN Deep groove, or single row radial, ball bearings are the most widely used bearings. They utilize an uninterrupted raceway that makes them optimal for radial loads. This design permits precision tolerance, even at high-speed operation. NTN ball bearings use standard cages of pressed steel, as well as machined, brass cages for high-speed applications. NTN also offers bearings with locating snap rings.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>NTN Deep Groove Ball BearingsNTN Prelubricated Deep Groove Ball Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Prelubricated bearings have integral seals, or shields, which are packed with long-life grease. In many applications, these bearings can be used without supplementary seals, closures, or protective devices.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>NTN TMB Deep Groove Ball Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>NTN TMB Bearings feature a lighter Weight and longer Life— Thermal Mechanical Ball Bearing (TMB) features: Clean, vacuum-degassed, high-carbon chromium steel that undergoes a proprietary heat treatment to reduce crack sensitivity; High-power density that allows a smaller bearing to support higher loads; Low-weight, compact designs; A wide range of sizes.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>NTN Angular Contact Ball Bearings</strong></li>
<li><strong>NTN Angular Contact Ball BearingsNTN Single Row Angular Contact Ball Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Single row angular contact ball bearings feature raceways with high and low shoulders. These opposing raceways are designed to carry thrust load in one direction.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>NTN Double Row Angular Contact Ball Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Double row angular contact ball bearings have an inner and outer ring with a double raceway. The two rows are so related that the contact angle is similar to a pair of back-to-back single row bearings.</p>

<li><strong>NTN Insulated Ball Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>NTN Insulated Ball Bearings offer two options to prevent electrical pitting damage. Double row angular contact ball bearings have an inner and outer ring with a double raceway. The two rows are so related that the contact angle is similar to a pair of back-to-back single row bearings.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>NTN Coated Insulated Ball Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>NTN Insulated Ball BearingsNTN’s MEGAOHM™ Series of insulated bearings has been specifically designed to counteract electrical pitting. These bearings are available in both ceramic and resin versions that are suited to a variety of applications. Coating the outer ring of the bearing provides a barrier (capable of resisting at least 100 M) at 500 VDC) against any stray currents that would pass through the bearing.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>NTN Ceramic Hybrid Insulated Ball Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>For especially problematic applications, NTN offers ceramic hybrid bearings. Using conventional steel inner and outer rings, silicon nitride rolling elements provide excellent electrical insulation while reducing rotating mass and improving heat dissipation.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>NTN Speciality Ball Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>NTN Specialty Ball BearingsDouble-row, self-aligning bearings from NTN utilize an inner ring with a single spherical raceway. In this configuration, the inner and outer rings can be misaligned relative to each other. The resulting effect is a comparatively large single angle that imposes moment loads upon the balls. The boundary dimensions of self-aligning bearings can be the same as standard ball bearings.</p>

<p>The rolled steel microstructure is more consistent than cast iron or cast steel, reducing the risk of housing fracture under severe conditions.</p>
<p>Rolled steel housing dimensions of the NTN Bearing Units Steel Series are consistent with cast units, allowing them to be interchanged with NTN standard housings and other manufacturers ISO standard.</p>
<p>NTN rolled steel housings provide superior strength to cast steel and cast iron. Their ability to resist impact loads makes them suitable for applications involving heavy loads and vibration. Possible applications for NTN rolled steel housings include but are not limited to conveyors, trucks and overhead cranes at steel mills, mining machinery and pollution control equipment.</p>
