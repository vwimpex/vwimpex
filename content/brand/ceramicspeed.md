<p>In 2000, Jacob Csizmadia introduced ceramic bearings to professional cycling. From 2001 they are producing hybrid bearings in Holstebro, Denmark, for both sports and industrial applications.<br> Hybrid bearings are made with top-grade silicon nitride ceramic balls, making them 58% lighter and 30-50% faster than traditional steel bearings.</p>
