<p>Glycodur has been manufacturing bearings with layers of self-lubricating material which require either no lubrication at all or only when initially installed.<br> Their commitment to preserving the environment extends beyond reducing the need for lubrication systems to include not only the material itself, free from lead, but also our solvent- free manufacturing processes. Through the years they have accumulated comprehensive knowledge regarding the manufacture as well as the application of Glycodur bearings. Continuous product development and strict quality control have led to a great number of satisfied, regular customers. </p>
