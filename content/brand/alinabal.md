<p><strong>Alinabal </strong>is a highly diversified, Global provider of Spherical Rod End Bearings, connecting linkages and assemblies, precision stampings, complex mechanical assemblies, special purpose printers, optical shutters and aircraft instrumentation. <strong>Alinabal</strong> motion transfer devices group produces a broad range of standard and custom rod ends, spherical bearings, linkage assemblies and stamped molded bearing assemblies.</p>
<p><br>
<strong>Alinabal Standard Rod Ends</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>A Series Rod Ends feature two-piece construction, steel on steel.</li>
<li>V Series Rod Ends feature four-piece construction, oil impregnated phosphor bronze race.</li>
<li>VX Series Precision Rod Ends feature four-piece construction, oil impregnated phosphor bronze race, with precision bore tolerance.</li>
<li>K Series Rod Ends feature economical stamped steel housing, steel ball.</li>
<li>KP Series Rod Ends feature economical stamped steel housing, steel ball, nylon race.</li>
<li>P Series Rod Ends feature steel housing and ball with injection molded nylon race.</li>
<li>Alumi-Lite Forged Rod Ends feature rugged forged aluminum 3-piece design.</li>
<li>Steel Pro-Line Rod Ends feature steel housing and ball, fully staked construction with carbon fiber reinforced nylon race.</li>
<li>Aluminum Pro-Line Rod Ends feature aluminum housing, steel ball, fully staked construction with carbon fiber reinforced nylon race.</li>
<li>Alinabal Special or Custom Rod Ends or S-Series feature full enclosure, and are studded and booted.</li>
<li>Alinabal High-Capacity Rod Ends feature high-capacity chrome moly steel housing, ball and race.</li>
<li>Large Bore Bearing RM/RF include large bore series 1" and larger rod end and spherical bearings.</li>
<li>ISO-LINK® Rod Ends are elastomeric rod ends for noise and vibration reduction.<br>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Alinabal Standard Spherical Bearings</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Extra Light Section Spherical Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>CBB Series bearings have the same construction features as the CBA Series bearings, but with reduced dimensions, resulting in a maximum capacity two-piece spherical bearing, with the minimum cross section related to bore size.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Light Section Spherical Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>CBA Series, NBA Series and PBL Series together form Alinabal's light spherical bearings line. The CBA Series features a single fractured race and a self-lubricating ball. The ball and race are precision ground to assure maximum bearing surface in the load zone</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Commercial Series Spherical Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>COM Series is dimensionally the same as the CBA series, but features a solid outer race. This series is also available with a Teflon-lined race for a bearing with minimal clearance and extended wear.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Heavy Section Spherical Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>VBC Series bearings combine a plated case hardened steel ball with a self lubricating sintered phosphor bronze race secured permanently in a low carbon, steel plated housing. These are an excellent general purpose spherical bearing.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>CRS Spherical Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Steel ball, heat treated & chrome plated with low carbon steel, oil coated race.</p>
