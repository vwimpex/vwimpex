<p>Oil seal supplier NBR, VITON etc oil seal importer. A2Z Oil seals are used to protect shafts and bearings from the ingress of dirt and foreign matter and the egress of oil or grease. SOG Oil Seals generally consists of an outer rubber (NBR / Viton ? Silicon) or metal part and an inner flexible member that does the actual sealing and is bonded to the metal part by chemical adhesive agents.<br>
<br>
<strong>Shaft Seals</strong></p>
<p>Oil seals are an important part of any moving and rotating part assembly. They are heavily used in hydraulic cylinders, gearboxes, etc.</p>
<p><strong>Construction of an SOG Oil Seals</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>The inward skeleton consists of a metal ring, which gives it an auxiliary soundness</li>
<li>Nitrile elastic makes up the external skin</li>
<li>The lip of the oil seal has a spring that prevents grease from spilling outside and outside contaminants from entering inside</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Materials used for oil seal’s external skin:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Silicone</li>
<li>Nitrile elastic</li>
<li>Poly acrylate</li>
<li>PolytetraFluroEthylene (PTFE)</li>
<li>Fluoroelastomer, also known as Viton</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>V-Seals</strong></p>
<p>The V-Seal is an all rubber seal for dirt exclusion, that mounts directly on the shaft and seals axially against the counterface, housing, bearing race, seal case or similar surfaces.We have VA, VS and VE type... etc.<br>
<br>
<strong>Wiper Seals</strong></p>
<p>Wiper seals are used for dirt exclusion. Typical applications are excavator equipments, lift trucks and hydraulic presses. Wiper seals are made of nitrile rubber, general in 90 shore A, for press-in fitting in open housings.</p>
