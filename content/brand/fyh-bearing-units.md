<p>FYH is the oldest and largest manufacturer of mounted ball bearing units in Japan. They began production of industrial mounted units for the Japanese market in 1950, and since then, have FYH Bearings Groupbecome a leading manufacturer of mounted units worldwide.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>FYH Ball Bearing Units</strong> are constructed of high-carbon chromium bearing steel and have precision honed raceways and riveted steel cages.</li>
<li><strong>Dust Resistant Units</strong> feature variety of supplemental sealing options FYH Ceramic Ball Bearingsthat function extremely well at excluding contamination.</li>
<li><strong>FYH's Original Ceramic Balls are hybrid bearing </strong>units for special environments.</li>
</ul>
<p>The <strong>NAP300E series are heavy duty units </strong>with eccentric locking collars. They serve as drop in replacements for RAO units and GN inserts.</p>
<p>FYH offers a variety of Mounted Units, including customized mounted beaings.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>FYH Mounted Bearing The BulletPoint </strong>- FYH's Original Setscrew - is a one piece ball point setscrew designed for normal use all to settings characterized by severe vibration, shock load and high speed. The innovative FYH Bullet Point is designed to expand the threads of the screw as the point of the screw contacts the surface of the shaft.</li>
</ul>
<p>The NU-LOC Series is produced with the specifications of the S3 designation as a standard which is desirable for high speed applications. NU-LOC bearings have tighter bore tolerance and internal clearance which are features that greatly reduce noise and vibration. Though they are high-precision bearings for demanding applications they are also suitable for a variety of other operating conditions. Nu-Loc NU Concentric-Loc Units feature easy Installation; 360°Shaft Contact; and a True Concentric Lock.</p>
