<p><strong>Intecom</strong> is a leading company in the production and selling of rolling bearings, having a dynamic and always updated catalogue that offers all the different types of product.<br> The company is also specialised in the production of spare part bearings for fork lifts with small and big capacities, and in the design of special bearings for general handling.<br> The very flexible structure of Intecom allows us to design, manufacture and sell small and big quantities of rolling bearings with balls or rollers.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Fixed combined bearings for laminated standard U profiles</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>INTECOM full complement ball fixed combined bearings are particularly suitable to be used in fork lift masts and in any other moving and handling system, where laminated or extruded profiles are used.<br> The excellent combination of radial-axial part allows high load capacity with extremely small dimensions, in addition to assembly easiness on any type of structure.U steel laminated profiles</p>
<ul>
<li>
<strong>Steel laminated profiles </strong>are used in different industrial sectors, where a linear mouvemnt is necessary. They are also used for fork lift masts, but also in the food, textile and automotive industry.</li>
</ul>
<p>Within these profiles, slide all the types of combined bearings with centred (not inclined) external profile, both with little and big dimensions.</p>
