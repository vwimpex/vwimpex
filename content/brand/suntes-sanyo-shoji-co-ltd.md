<p>Suntes (Sanyo Shoji Co., Ltd.) is a Japanese company specializing in the design, manufacture, and sale of power transmission and control equipment, such as brakes and clutches. Established in 1961, the company is a leading manufacturer of elevator brakes and also provides products for industries like machine tools, steelworks, and port cranes. Their products are used to ensure safety and efficiency in various applications worldwide. </p>
<p><strong>Main products</strong>: Power transmission and control equipment, including:</p>
<ul>
<li>Elevator brakes (high-rise buildings, etc.)</li>
<li>Machine tool brakes</li>
<li>Clutches</li>
<li>Brakes for port and container cranes</li>
<li>Various industrial and construction equipment brakes.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Key information</strong></p>
<p><strong>Specialization</strong>: Suntes is a top manufacturer of special brakes in Japan, with a focus on power control technology.</p>
<p><strong>Applications:</strong> Their products are used in a wide range of applications to improve safety and efficiency, such as:</p>
<ul>
<li>Transportation (elevators, port equipment)</li>
<li>Machine tools</li>
<li>Steel plants</li>
<li>Cranes (container, climbing)</li>
<li>Water gates and pumps.</li>
</ul>
