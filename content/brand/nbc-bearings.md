<p>NBC Bearings is a product of National Engineering Industries Ltd (NEI), India’s leading bearings manufacturer and exporter of a wide range of bearings for the automotive, industrial and railways. The company was founded by Mr. B M Birla in 1946 in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India.</p>
<p>Founded in 1946, NBC is a pioneer, renowned for excellence in quality and delivery for over 60 years. An integral part of the US$ 1.6Bn CK Birla Group, NBC produces over 150 million bearings each year in more than 1000 sizes to serve a host of varied customers across India and 29 other countries in 5 continents.</p>
<p>NEI manufactures bearings in about 1000 different sizes. The size of bearings manufactured by NBC ranges from 25 mm to 1.3 meter Outer diameter.</p>
<p>The company has three manufacturing facilities located at Jaipur, Newai and Manesar and Savli (Vadodara).</p>
<p>NBC’s current makes 150 million bearings annually across its four plants.</p>
