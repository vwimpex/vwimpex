<p>RINGSPANN was founded in 1944 and is headquartered in Bad Homburg near Frankfurt, Germany. RINGSPANN is a leading technology manufacturer in freewheels, industrial brakes, shaft-hub-connections, overload clutches, couplings and precision clamping fixtures. The company has eight manufacturing facilities in Germany and abroad.</p>
