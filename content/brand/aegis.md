<p><strong>AEGIS® PRO Series High Current Bearing Protection Ring</strong></p>
<p>The AEGIS® PRO Series design provides reliable shaft grounding for medium voltage applications, generators, and turbines to divert harmful shaft voltages to ground and extend bearing life. Install the AEGIS® PRO on the DE and insulate the bearing on the opposite end (NDE) for best results. Large motors and generators often have much higher induced shaft voltages and bearing currents. The six circumferential rows of conductive microfber provide the extra protection for these high current applications.</p>
<p><strong>Motor with AEGIS Bearing Protection Ring</strong></p>
<p>The AEGIS Shaft Grounding Ring prevents electrical bearing fluting damage by safely diverting harmful shaft voltages and bearing currents to ground. Using proprietary Electron Transport Technology, the conductive microfibers inside the AEGIS Ring provide the most reliable current diversion technology for shaft grounding with a "path of least resistance" to dramatically extend motor life.</p>
