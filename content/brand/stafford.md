<p>Founded in 1975, Stafford Manufacturing Corp. is built on commitment to quality products, superior customer service and employee development. Arthur Stafford started the company on a shoestring budget but with a clear vision of what was required to accomplish those goals.</p>
<p><strong>Stafford Manufacturing Corp.</strong> is a manufacturer and distributor of shaft collars, rigid shaft couplings and specialty mechanical components.</p>
<p>They’re used in a wide range of industrial and consumer OEM and MRO applications, including:</p>
<ul>
<li>Power transmission</li>
<li>Linear motion</li>
<li>Motion control</li>
<li>Fixturing</li>
<li>Material handling</li>
<li>Display</li>
<li>Automation</li>
<li>Assembly</li>
</ul>
<p>They manufacture standard and custom shaft collars that can accommodate the requirements and restrictions of virtually any industrial or consumer application. Their extensive selection of shaft collar options include numerous design (e.g., one-piece, two-piece, and threaded), material (e.g., metal and plastic), and finishing (e.g., black oxide and chrome plating) choices. For round bore shaft collars alone, they offer 78 different options.</p>
<p><strong>Product Categories :</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Round Bore Shaft Collars</li>
<li>Rigid Shaft Couplings & Adaptors</li>
<li>Threaded Bore Shaft Couplings</li>
<li>Square & Hex Bore Couplings</li>
<li>Mounting Systems</li>
<li>Metric Collars & Couplings</li>
<li>Flanged Devices</li>
<li>ACCU-CLAMP Collars & Couplings</li>
</ul>
