<p>PFI Bearings is a dynamic American company with a global footprint and over 25 years manufacturing experience producing highest quality bearings.</p>
<p>PFI Bearings can be used for agricultural, industrial and automotive (light, medium and heavy-duty) applications and their main bearing lines are alternator and starter bearings, A/C compressor bearings, clutch release bearings, transmission bearings, electrical motor bearings, differential bearings, tensioner bearings and wheel bearings.</p>
