<p>Since 1947, Bearings Limited has been dedicated to providing bearing and power transmission distributors with high quality products, competitive pricing and a level of service unmatched in the industry.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Ball Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Bearings Limited offers a wide variety of radial and angular contact ball bearings, as well as linear motion bearings and a complete line of spherical plain bearings (ball bushings). Popular bearing sizes are available in both premium and secondary brands, giving distributors multiple options for filling their customers’ needs – all with a single purchase order.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Mounted Units & Inserts</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Bearings Limited manufactories a complete line of TRITAN ball bearing pillow blocks, flanges and take-up units. All come in a wide variety of configurations and materials, including cast iron, ductile iron, stainless steel, thermoplastic and nickel-plated. Also available are roller bearing split housings (plummer blocks) with adapters.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Power Transmission (Chain, Sprockets, Bushings Sheaves/Pulleys & Gears)</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Bearings Limited is proud to produce TRITAN Power Transmission Products. TRITAN produces world class power transmission products using select alloy steel, advanced quality control and testing systems to ensure the highest quality.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Roller Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>An extensive selection of roller bearings are available including spherical roller bearings, cylindrical roller bearings, needle bearings and tapered roller bearings. Popular sizes are supplied in both premium & secondary brands.</p>
