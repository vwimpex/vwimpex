<p>Rexnord, a leading manufacturer of critical system components, including gear drives, bearings, chain, and couplings, engineers its products to meet cost and performance criteria and ensure the most efficient solution for specific applications.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Rexnord Roller Bearings:</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>For 100 years Rex and Link-Belt® roller bearings have been built to survive the toughest applications in the harshest environments. Rexnord's roller bearings feature superior sealing and shaft mounting technology, performance surfaces precision-ground and finished in the USA, as well as high thrust and radial load capacities. We offer dedicated engineering support to help customize units to your specifications. From high speeds to punishing loads, indoors to underwater, Rex and Link-Belt roller bearings take abuse and come back for more.</p>

<li><strong>Rexnord Ball Bearings:</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Rexnord® offers the broadest range of ball bearings in industry. With a legacy of performance and quality, LinkBelt brand and MBSM brand ball bearings have become trusted leaders for applications requiring a ball bearing that will lend uptime, quality and performance to your application. Our ball bearings are proudly used in Agrifarm, Construction Equipment, Air Handling, Food and Beverage among other great industries and applications. Those who recognize the importance of keeping their application running smoothly value the dependability and quality you can count on with LinkBelt and MB Ball Bearings.</p>
