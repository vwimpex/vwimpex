</ul>
<p>Ease of use and high reliability make UNIMEC trapezoidal screw jacks suitable for a wide variety of uses. UNIMEC trapezoidal screw jacks are absolutely irreversible, that is, they can support their applied loads without needing any brakes or other locking systems.</p>
