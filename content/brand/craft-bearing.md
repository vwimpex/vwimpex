<p><b>Craft Split Bearing Co</b> is a Virginia-based manufacturer of split cylindrical roller bearings and a recognized leader in split bearing technology. The great advantage of a split bearing is its ease of replacement in the event of a bearing failure. Because the bearing is split down the shaft so that all components, even the sels, can be easily replaced, the task of replacing a bearing becomes a minor one.</p>
<p>The Craft Split Bearing is not only durable and rugged but contains many innovative features for which Craft has received patents. For example, all of the Craft pillow block bearings have a standard base-to-center height (although custom, metric, and large-bore bearings are also available).</p>
<p>Features of the Craft Split Bearing Pillow Block (Housing Cap)</p>
<p>Houses either an expansion (FL) or non-expansion bearing (HD). Inside diameter is spherically machined to accept the cartridge assembly, allowing the pillow block to compensate for misalignment up to 3%. Base-to-center height meets industry standards.</p>
<p><b>Cartridge</b></p>
<p>The spherically machined cartridge takes initial shaft misalignment externally, allowing the bearing and seals to remain parallel to the shaft at all times. Each cartridge comes drilled, tapped, and plugged in its lower half so that lubricant can be purged.</p>
<p><b>Outer Race</b></p>
<p>Made of 52100 steel, hardened to a Rockwell COF 59/62. The outer race joints are cut in a chevron so that the roller is carried across the split. The outer race comes in either a FLOAT (FL) or HELD (HD) type.</p>
<p><b>Aluminum Triple Labyrinth Seals</b></p>
<p>Craft's triple-labyrinth seals, unlike felt or lip type seals, rotate with the shaft eliminating and chance of shaft wear caused by the seal.</p>
<p><b>Inner Race</b></p>
<p>Made of 52100 steel, hardened to a Rockwell C 59/62. The split of the inner race is cut at a precise angle so that when the rollers cross the split they are carried across, having only point contact with the split.</p>
<p><b>Clamp Collars</b></p>
<p>Made of 4142 alloy and thru hardened rather than flame hardened. The inside lips of the clamp collars form the running track of the rollers and act as guides for the rollers. The Clamp Collars also assist the HD (Held) bearings in accepting axial loads.</p>
<p><b>Pillow Block Pedestal Housing</b></p>
<p>Like the cartridge, the split pedestal housing is made of high-quality, strong ductile iron and can be easily disassembled (by removing two bolts) for bearing replacement.</p>
