<p> "Nippon Kodo Ltd." - Automation Technology is a company that registered its own trade mark NIKO in Japan and has manufacturing facilities throughout Asia. It produces elements for rotary and linear motion systems. Headquartered in Kyoto, it is aimed at the industrial market to offer reliable quality, proposing a catalogue including supports, deep-groove radial ball bearings, roller bearings, tapered rollers bearings as well as junctions, linear ball bushing.</p>
