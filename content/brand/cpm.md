<p>CPM SpA is a solid Italian industrial reality, specialized in production, in medium to high quantities, of special rolling bearings, highly customized, in a dimensional range between 10 mm bore and 800 mm external diameter. The special ball bearings, cylindrical roller bearings, tapered roller bearings and needle roller bearings that CPM manufactures, are used in many different fields of application all over the world. The bearings designed by CPM are all developed “tailor made”, fulfilling the requests customers in their specific application, from automotive to robotics, from mechanical power transmissions to textile machines.</p>
<p>The main type of bearings that we manufacture are:</p>
<ul>
<li>
<strong>Radial ball bearings</strong></li>
<li><strong> Angular contact ball bearings</strong></li>
<li><strong> Four point contact ball bearings</strong></li>
<li><strong> Thin section ball bearings</strong></li>
<li><strong> Self-aligning ball bearings</strong></li>
<li><strong> Slewing rings</strong></li>
<li><strong> Cylindrical roller bearings</strong></li>
<li><strong> Crossed-roller bearings</strong></li>
<li><strong> Needle roller bearings</strong></li>
<li><strong> Tapered roller bearings</strong></li>
<li><strong> Track bearings</strong></li>
<li><strong> U-joint bearings</strong></li>
<li><strong> Precision rings</strong></li>
</ul>
