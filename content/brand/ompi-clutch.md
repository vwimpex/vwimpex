<p>In 1976 OMPI was set up, originally under the name of Officina Meccanica Pinzoni, in a small shed on the northern outskirts of Milan.</p>
<p>OMPI is a family-owned company, which, as to cope with the continuous growth and expansion on new markets worldwide, expanded, widening the number and quality of machinery and increasing its personnel. At present it employs over 250 employees and it manages production facilities in Italy, Turkey, China and India.</p>
