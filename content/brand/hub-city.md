<p><strong>Hub City</strong> is one of the world's largest manufacturers of heavy duty power transmission products. From gear drives and speed reducers to couplings and bearings, each Hub City line of products is manufactured to the highest level of quality and engineered with 120 years of industry knowledge.</p>
<p><strong>Hub City</strong> offers power transmission products for applications in recreation, transportation, military, packaging, construction, communication, material handling, medical and food processing equipment.</p>
<p><strong>Hub City's</strong> major product Lines are: Bearings, Electric Motors, Farm Products & Accessories, Gear Drives, Gearmotors, High Efficiency Helical Drives, Speed Reducers, Washdown Products and Custom Products.</p>
<p>More than half of Hub's products are either modified or completely customized. One of the greatest benefits of doing business with Hub City is the strength of its engineering capabilities. Hub City prides itself in filling its customers' needs with cost effective solutions.</p>
