<p><br>
<strong>ENC</strong> temperature bearings are specially designed for work applications in high temperature conditions.</p>
<p>All our ENC bearings are made of AISI 52100 steel and incorporate the most suitable greases for each thermal grade as a lubrication solution.</p>
<p> ENC temperature bearings are especially useful for applications in conditions subject to high temperatures such as trucks used in kilns for brick making, in furnaces for the ceramic industry, in rollers in working conditions at elevated temperatures in the steel manufacturing industry, aluminum manufacturing, glass manufacturing, Steel industry, Etc.</p>
<ul>
<li>HIGH TEMPERATURE BEARINGS ENC150</li>
</ul>
<p>Our ENC150 bearings are made of AISI 52100 steel with C4 radial clearance and greased for temperatures from -29º C up to 170ºC. 150º Vulcanized rubber protection.</p>
<ul>
<li>HIGH TEMPERATURE BEARINGS ENC200</li>
</ul>
<p>Our ENC20 bearings are made of AISI 52100 steel with C4 radial clearance and greased for temperatures from -20º C up to 265ºC. ZZ shields.</p>
<ul>
<li>HIGH TEMPERATURE BEARINGS ENC250</li>
</ul>
<p>Our ENC250 bearings are made of AISI 52100 steel with C4 radial clearance and greased -20º C up to 265ºC. 2RS-265C Vulcanized rubber protection.</p>
<ul>
<li>HIGH TEMPERATURE BEARINGS ENC270C</li>
</ul>
<p>Our UC270C bearings are made of AISI 52100 steel with a Manganese Phosphate surface treatment, Increased radial clearance to compensate for termal expansion, greased with KlüberBarrierta LT 55/2 grease from -30º C to 270ºC. With ZZ shields.</p>
<ul>
<li>HIGH TEMPERATURE BEARINGS UC270CZZ</li>
</ul>
<p>Our UC270C bearings are made of AISI 52100 steel with a Manganese Phosphate surface treatment, Increased radial clearance to compensate for termal expansion, greased with Molyguard CF-2 grease from -35º C to 270ºC.</p>
<ul>
<li>HIGH TEMPERATURE BEARINGS ENC330</li>
</ul>
<p>Our enc330 bearings are made of AISI 52100 steel with a Manganese Phosphate surface treatment, Increased radial clearance to compensate for termal expansion, greased with Klüber’s Wolfrasin ULAF 60 grease up to 330ºC. ZZ and open format available .</p>
