<p>Established in 1989, <strong>MAV</strong> located in Altopiano della Vigolana (Bosentino) in Northern Italy is one of the world’s Largest Manufacturer of Locking Assemblies renowned for their creativity & Capacity to offer entire range of Locking Assemblies.</p>
<p><strong>MAV</strong> specializes in designing and manufacturing special items according to customer application and design requirements. We manufacture keyless locking devices, external shrink discs, and rigid couplings for shafts ranging from 5 mm to more than 1 meter. This unprecedented product breadth includes more than 40 series available in both metric and imperial bores.</p>
<p><b>Standard Locking Devices –<br> MAV Internal Locking Assemblies</b></p>
<p>Also known as clamping units, MAV internal locking assemblies mount inside the hub. As the screws are tightened, the resulting high radial forces create an interference fit that is a zero-backlash connection between the shaft and hub.</p>
<p>MAV locking assemblies use tapers that generate high radial forces when pushed together, creating high contact pressure and a zero-backlash connection. The locking assemblies mount gears, pulleys, cams, levers, rotors, and similar devices onto shafts, while our rigid couplings connect two shafts. MAV devices allow excellent centering between hub and shaft and are well balanced. Because of the high radial forces produced during installation, MAV devices can handle heavy torsional, axial, radial, or bending loads.</p>
<p><b>MAV Stainless Steel</b></p>
<p>All MAV units are available in stainless steel, including martensitic or austenitic stainless steel. Zinc plating, nickel plating, and phosphate treatment also are available. Please note, however, that any plating or treatment will impact performance. Our technical team can help you with the correct selection for your application.</p>
<p><b>MAV Mini Series</b></p>
<p>All MAV keyless locking device products are available in smaller sizes (starting from 5 mm) — typical sizes for packaging machines, medical devices, and linear guides.</p>
<p><b>MAV Rigid Couplings</b></p>
<p>MAV rigid shaft couplings connect two shafts, transferring rotary motion from a driving shaft to a driven shaft. Simple and durable, MAV rigid shaft couplings transmit more power than comparable flexible shaft couplings. Unable to compensate for misalignment, they must be used in applications where the shafts are aligned. MAV rigid couplings are commonly used in automation, motion control, power transmission, as well as original equipment manufacturer (OEM) and maintenance, repair, and operation (MRO) applications.</p>
<p><b>Shrink Discs –<br> MAV External Locking Devices</b></p>
<p>MAV shrink discs are installed externally on a hollow shaft or hub to connect it to a shaft, for example— in a hollow-shaft gearbox. When the shrink disc’s locking screws are tightened, the thrust rings are drawn together and compress the inner ring. This creates an interference fit by applying pressure to the outside of the hub, thus clamping it onto the shaft. Shrink discs are the preferred alternative to conventional shrink, shrink/fit/press fit, tapered bushings or keyways.</p>
<p>Because the force applied to the hub is always within the elastic limit of the material, the hub springs back when the force is released, making MAV external locking devices easy to remove.</p>
