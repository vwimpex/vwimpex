<p><strong>FK Bearings</strong> Incorporated, a World-Class, leading USA manufacturer of rod ends, spherical bearings, custom-engineered bearings and related products. Established in 1983, FK Bearings brings over 35 years of experience to the industrial and performance racing and off-road markets.</p>
<p>Today, FK Bearings has grown to become the preferred source for motion control products to nearly every industry. Our products are utilized in various applications in: construction equipment, mining equipment, material handling, transportation and off-highway equipment, automation and robotics, machine tools, agricultural equipment, military vehicles, performance racing, and many others.</p>
<p>FK Bearings corporate headquarters and state-of-the-art manufacturing facility is located in Southington, Connecticut. </p>
