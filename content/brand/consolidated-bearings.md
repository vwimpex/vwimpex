<p>Consolidated Bearings Company (Consolidated) is a leading importer and wholesaler of bearings and related products, providing our customers access to the world's best manufacturers from one reliable source.</p>
<p>Established in 1932 by William F. Meerwarth. More than eighty-five years later, we have remained steadfast in our mission to always put the customer first. Today, Consolidated continues to lead the industry due to our constant dedication and ongoing investment in customer service and quality products.</p>
<p>Consolidated is a third-generation, family-owned business that continues to play an integral role in the global bearing market.</p>
