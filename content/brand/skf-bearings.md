<p><b>SKF Group</b> is the leading global supplier of products, solutions and services within rolling bearings, seals, mechatronics, services and lubrication systems. The company was founded in Sweden in 1907 - the same year as the ball bearing became a commercial product.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong><a href="https://www.ebatmus.com/skf_rolling_bearings.html">SKF Bearing Products</a></strong></li>
</ul>
<p>SKF has a wide selection of bearings including Rolling bearings, High-/super-precision bearings; Bearing units; Bearing housings; Spherical plain bearings and rod ends; and Composite dry sliding bearings and FW bushings.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong><a href="https://www.ebatmus.com/skf_seals.html">SKF Seals</a></strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Seals and sealing technology are essential parts of the capabilities of SKF. Seals from SKF stand for excellence and leadership and symbolize consistent endeavour to achieve total quality in all processes. SKF offers the following types of seals: Automotive seals; Fork seals; Axial shaft seals; Hydraulic seals; Radial shaft seals: Static seals; Wear sleeves; and Machined Seals.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong><a href="https://www.ebatmus.com/skf_bearing_lubrication.html">SKF Bearing Lubricants</a></strong></li>
</ul>
<p>The strict standards and testing parameters developed and applied at the SKF Engineering and Research Centre have become internationally recognised benchmarks for bearing greases. The comprehensive range of SKF bearing greases is the result of many decades of research and development. Each individual lubricant is precisely adjusted to the respective field of application.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong><a href="https://www.ebatmus.com/skf_mechatronics.html">SKF Mechatronics</a></strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Mechatronics is the science of bringing together mechanics and electronics into exciting new solutions. It is a rapidly advancing area that provides dramatic opportunity to solve old problems in a clever and environmentally friendly way. SKF has more than 400 engineers dedicated to this area.</p>
<p>By being at the forefront of this technology for many years, SKF has developed unique application knowledge in hardware and software. In combining actuators, bearings, sealing, lubrication, sensors and electronics competences, we have been able to develop a number of standard and customized solutions. SKF offers a wide assortment of mechatronic products.</p>
