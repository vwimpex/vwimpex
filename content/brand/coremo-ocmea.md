<p><strong>Coremo Brakes and Clutches</strong></p>
<p>COREMO OCMEA, manufactures Mechanical, Pneumatic or Hydraulic Brakes and Clutches for the industries such as Marine, Oil & Gas, Wire & Cable, Rubber & Tyre, Energy and Entertainment sector applications and for all other industrial applications. COREMO OCMEA, established 1960, is based in Assago – Milan (Italy)</p>
<p><strong>Pneumatic & Manual Caliper Brakes</strong></p>
<p>Pneumatic Caliper Brakes are designed for applications which use air pressure to operate the braking system. All Coremo Caliper brakes can be fitted with a wear indicator and an on/off indicator device to protect the system during operation. The brake can be installed in any position since it has a lever balancing and lining pad alignment system (depending on type).</p>
<p><strong>Hydraulic Brakes</strong></p>
<p>Hydraulic Brakes are designed to provide a simple and fast solution to the safety requirements of a wide range of industrial applications. Available with levered or direct thruster, either oil or spring applied, these brakes are designed to allow multiple configurations and maximum customization.</p>
<p><strong>Small Pneumatic Brakes & Clutches</strong></p>
<p>SB&TB brakes are combinable pneumatic braking units that provide an efficient and reconfigurable system over time. Available in both positive and negative versions for safety applications, these brakes are distinguished by their high versatility and simplicity during assembly and maintenance.</p>
<p><strong>Fan Cooled Brakes</strong></p>
<p>"Modulo" Brakes: high fan ventilation, high thermal dissipation and Kevlar pads. These brakes are particularly suitable for unwinding and winding applications with maximum speeds of 2,670 rpm.</p>
<p><strong>Air Tube Design Clutches & Brakes </strong></p>
<p><strong>Water Cooled Brakes</strong></p>
<p>Water cooled high heat dissipation brakes and clutches: water cooling system + high heat dissipation + multidisc system + air tube design. High Torque Range up to 51.000 Nm</p>
<p><strong>Double Ventilated Brakes.</strong></p>
<p>Double ventilated disc and special membrane sensitive to slightest pressure changes. Braking force 0.25[Nm] to 1120[Nm].</p>
<p><strong>DISCS</strong></p>
<p>In its product range Coremo can supply cast iron hat discs as well as steel flat discs, with or without hub. Such discs are available in standard version or customized in dimensions and machining.</p>
<p><strong>COUPLINGS</strong></p>
<p>Gear Oscillating Couplings + Elastic Couplings + Flexible Disc Couplings</p>
