<p><strong>Ziller GmbH & Co</strong>. KG has been producing metal seals for roller bearings, the NILOS ring, for more than 60 years. The NILOS ring is the ideal solution for your sealing problem.</p>
<p>NILOS-Rings are metal seals, designed to keep dirt, dust, debris and other abrasive contaminants out of ball and roller bearings.NILOS-Rings help extend bearing uptime in the harshest operating environments and industries. Precision-manufactured for robust, durability and standard, NILOS-Rings are produced from Galvanized steel, Stainless steel and Brass.Galvanized steel is especially good in environments where there are tough abrasive particles. Stainless steel is usually used in food processing environments, and other applications where water or other corrosive materials are present. Brass is used in applications where there are high concentrations of salt or other corrosive solutions.</p>
<p>NILOS-Rings can accommodate almost any ball, cylindrical roller, taper roller or spherical roller bearing having standard ISO dimensions.They can also be used with a range of single and double-row bearing types.They can be used as primary seals (i.e. the only seal) or secondary (additional) seals.</p>
<p>NILOS rings are available in a wide assortment of styles, materials and sealing configurations. In addition, there’s a Nilos ring available to fit almost any standard bearing size. The NILOS ring is a proven means to protect the bearing from contaminants that could reduce the life of the bearing.</p>
<p><strong>Two basic shapes of seals AV, JV:</strong></p>
<p>These two types of NILOS-Rings cover the majority of configurations used. The standard types of NILOS-Rings can be used for peripheral rotating speeds of up to 6 m/s; higher speeds must quoted without fail when ordering.</p>

<li><strong> AV: </strong>
</li>
</ul>
<p>A NILOS-Ring that seals the outer roller bearing ring is described as a type AV.<br> Description: An “externally-sealing” NILOS-Ring for a single-row 6410 AV deep groove ball bearing is described as type “6410 AV”.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>JV:</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>In design work the types of NILOS-Rings that seal the inner roller bearing ring should be used if at all possible. For preference, use the corresponding NILOS-Rings of type JV. Description: An “internally-sealing“ NILOS-Ring for a single-row 6410 deep groove ball bearing is described as type “6410 JV”.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>For Z and RS bearings ZAV, ZJV:</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>The sealing of bearings that have already been provided with shields or seals is done by NILOS-Rings of other dimensions so that the seal clamped into the bearing is fully encapsulated by the NILOS-Ring. NILOS-Rings are required in particular in such cases if dirt can penetrate up to the bearing or if condensation is formed.<br>
</p>

<li><strong>For taper roller bearings AV, JV, AK:</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>The two forms of NILOS-Ring, the AV and the JV, are produced for the bearing sides shown in the illustration. They are dimensioned to guarantee to be always in place on the roller bearing ring and thus ensure that proper sealing is achieved in all cases.</p>

<li><strong>Steel disk seal LSTO:</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Sealing package comprising of steel disks are ready for installation and capable of handling thrust loads. The sealing works on a non-contact basis and can follow axial movements of the roller bearing rings to 0.3 mm without any jamming of the disc.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Type LST-L steel disk seal:</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>The type LST-L ring is a further development of the type LSTO NILOS ring. By means of an additional Viton gasket, a considerable sealing effect is achieved when in contact with liquids.</p>
