<p>Fujian Longxi Bearing(Group) Corp., Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as “LS”) is a modernized state-owned listed company that mainly produces Spherical Plain Bearing(SPB), Tapered Roller Bearing, Rolling Components and High-end Machinery Parts. LS is also known as one of the Top 100 Enterprises of China Machinery Industry Core Competence, National Torch Program Key High-Tech Enterprises and National Innovation-oriented Pilot Enterprises. LS is the only one Single Champion Demonstration Enterprise in the China Bearing Manufacturing Industry, the National Model Enterprise in the cultivation of industrial brands, and the leading manufacturer of China SPB Industry.</p>
