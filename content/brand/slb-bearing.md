<p>SLB Manufactures deep groove ball bearing with wide and Narrow inner rings, consisting of insert bearings ( SA200, SB200, UC200, UEL200, UK200, UCX00 and UC300 ) and various housings. The types of bearing units are defined according to the different mounting methods of the bearings to shafts: the set-screws type, the adapter type, the eccentric locking collar type.</p>
<p>SLB housings are mainly casting housings. There are pressed steel plate housings as well align with ease during operation and can be conveniently mounted or dismounted. The bearing units can operate satisfactorily under working conditions, especially for machines operating in dusty or muddy surroundings. Thus, they are widely used in agricultural, construction and transmission machineries, etc. There are various types of sealing devices for our products, such as synthetic rubber seals, slinger with synthetic rubber seals and triple lip seals etc.</p>
