<p>Bones Bearings started when George Powell saw the need of skateboarders for faster bearings in the early 80's. After searcing for a good bearing manufacturer that would still be affordable to skateboarders.</p>
<p>George found a manufacturer in Switzerland that had bearings that were faster than any that they had at the time. He worked with them to design a bearing that would be ideal for skateboarders.<br> Here are currently six different models of bearings offered by Bones. Listed in decreasing order starting with the best.</p>
<ul>
<li> Bones Swiss Ceramics</li>
<li> Bones Swiss "L2"</li>
<li> Bones Super Swiss 6</li>
<li> Bones Swiss</li>
<li> Bones Ceramic Super Reds</li>
<li> Bones Super Reds</li>
<li> Bones Reds</li>
</ul>
