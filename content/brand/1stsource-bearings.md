<p>1stSource Products Inc. is an industrial hardware supplier that specializes in products such as Overhead Conveyors, Bearings, Ball Transfers, Skate Wheels, Fence Gate Hardware, and Non-Sparking Tools. The company is committed to delivering high-quality, reliable, and durable components to its customers. They emphasize a seamless procurement process and fast delivery. Their target clients include businesses in need of industrial hardware solutions. They have the ability to design, prototype and produce virtually any bearing related product required by the Conveyor Industry.</p> Products and services <ul>
<li>Conveyor systems and components: Products include flexible, overhead, and roller conveyors, as well as belts, chains, sprockets, and rollers.</li>
<li>Industrial hardware: They supply items like bearings, ball transfers, skate wheels, and non-sparking tools.</li>
<li>Fence and gate hardware: The company provides a range of hinges and other hardware for fences and gates.</li>
<li>Engineering and design: 1st Source Products offers 2D and 3D engineering services and computer simulations for material handling systems.</li>
<li>OEM parts: They supply parts for Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEM). </li>
</ul>
