<p>The Timken Company keeps the world turning with innovative friction management and power transmission products. Timken is one of the world's leading producers of highly engineered anti-friction bearings and alloy steel components, including being the bearing industry's leading supplier of 52100 steel. Other areas Timken specialize in are power transmission components, engineered surfaces and coatings, lubrication, seals and motion control systems.</p>
<p>Specifically, The Timken Company has several recognizable brands:</p>
<p>Timken tapered roller bearings are world renowned for their quality and breadth of sizes. Using case-carburized steel, Timken tapers allow for optimal performance in severe applications, including those with severe contamination and shock loads. Timken has the only offering of high precision Class 3, 0, and 00 machine tool grade tapered roller bearings.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Fafnir ball bearings </strong>are well known in the machine tool, aerospace, conveyor, and textile industries among others. Fafnir's plant in North Carolina manufactures precision angular contact ball bearings to world class standards, including hybrid bearings using ceramic balls for today's higher speed spindle applications. Fafnir also offers a wide variety of ball bearings including single row radial, angular contacts, double rows, and a full line of mounted units.</li>
<li><strong>Torrington</strong> full-complement needle bearings are the standard in the industry. In addition, Torrington offers a full-complement of roller bearings including spherical roller bearings used in SAF pillow block applications. Torrington's fractured spherical bushings are available in a wide variety of designs and sizes.</li>
<li><strong>Friction Management Solutions</strong> are now an important part of Timken's product offering. A full line of engineered greases and lubricators are now available for those hard-to-reach maintenance applications. And to assist with your preventative maintenance efforts, Timken's system, StatusCheck, allows for vibration and temperature monitoring of critical components.</li>
</ul>
