<p><br>
<strong>DV-B Drehverbindungen Bautzen GmbH</strong> designs and produces slewing bearings and gear rims that are use in many different and distinct sectors of industry. DV-B Drehverbindungen Bautzen GmbH Started in 2007 & extensive expertise and competence in the bearings industry due in large part to there sister company MBT Maschinenbauteile Scholze, which was founded in 1992.</p>
<p><strong>Products:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>L1 series (Single-row ball slewing bearings with flange)</li>
</ul>
<p>Single-row ball slewing bearings with flange are often referred to as the “light series”.</p>
<ul>
<li>K1 series (Single-row ball slewing bearings)</li>
</ul>
<p>Single-row ball slewing bearings are the most commonly used slewing bearings.</p>
<ul>
<li>K2 series (Double-row ball slewing bearings)</li>
</ul>
<p>DV-B double-row ball slewing bearings are usually manufactured as eight-point contact bearings with two symmetrical ball raceways.</p>
<ul>
<li>R3 series – Triple-row roller slewing bearings</li>
</ul>
<p>Slewing bearings in our R3 series have the highest static and dynamic load capacity thanks to their three roller raceways (one supporting, one retaining and one radial raceway).</p>
<ul>
<li>X1 series (Crossed-roller slewing bearings)</li>
</ul>
<p>All slewing bearings in the X1 series are crossed-roller bearings.</p>
<ol>
<li>Gear rims- Gear rims are toothed rings, which, in contrast to gear wheels, do not sit directly on an axle, but are usually screwed to a component with the help of fastening bores.</li>
<li>Pinions- In addition to gear rims and geared slewing bearings, we can design and supply the matching pinions following the load capacity calculation.</li>
</ol>
