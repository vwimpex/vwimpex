<p><strong>UNIBALL BEARINGS</strong> produces the industry Widest Range of Rod End.</p>
<p>In 2000, RBC acquired <strong>Schaublin SA</strong> based in Delémont, Switzerland. As a result, RBC added Schaublin’s metric rod ends and metric spherical bearings to the family of global RBC products, and a base with which to service the European market. Within this 140,000 sq. ft. facility, RBC has established the company’s European Distribution Center. In addition, <strong>Schaublin</strong> was licensed by Heim® Bearings to market <strong>Unibal® </strong>rod ends back in the 1950s.</p>
