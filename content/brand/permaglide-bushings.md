<strong>KS PERMAGLIDE® Plain bearing </strong></p>
<p><br>
<strong>Low-maintenance and maintenance-free plain bearings</strong></p>
<p>KS PERMAGLIDE embodies the product characteristic of permanently low-wear sliding. The use of solid lubricants in the plastic matrix makes it possible to use KS PERMAGLIDE® plain bearings from material group P1 (maintenance-free materials) without oil or grease lubrication.</p>
<p><strong>The unique features of KS PERMAGLIDE® P1:</strong></p>
<p> Universal use<br> Low friction values, high wear resistance<br> Extremely good performance in dry runs as self-lubricating</p>
