<p>Scheerer Bearing, a Pennsylvania bearing manufacturer, was incorporated on July 31, 1963. The history of Scheerer dates back to 1953, when Erich and Kurt Scheerer bought the Zubler Bearing Corp. (est. 1928 in Philadelphia). When the building was destroyed by a fire in 1963, Erich Scheerer and Pedro Rymar, then plant manager at Zubler, started what is today the Scheerer Bearing Corporation. In 1977, the Rymar family purchased the business from Erich Scheerer. The company’s objective is to offer a bearing product line that meets the specific needs of customers.</p>
<p>Scheerer Bearing, a third-generation, family-owned business, produces and carries a full line of ball and roller bearings up to 100 inches OD. Scheerer Bearings are high-quality large-size, custom and standard bearings.</p>
