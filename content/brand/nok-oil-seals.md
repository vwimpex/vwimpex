<ul>
<li>
<h2><strong>Oil Seals:</strong></h2>
</li>
</ul>
<p>Oil seals are functional parts used to seal oil. Composed of synthetic rubber, metal rings and springs, they prevent the leakage of lubricants from gaps within machines and bearings. They also prevent the entry of dust from the outside.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>O-Rings:</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>O-rings are O-shaped ring packings. They are appropriately compressed by fitting into grooves, and prevent leakage of various fluids, including oil, water, air and gas.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Hydraulic Packing:</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>"Packing" is a collective term for sealing devices that are mounted on the moving parts of hydraulic, pneumatic, and water-pressure equipment.</p>
