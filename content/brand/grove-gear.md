<p><strong>Grove Gear was founded as a contract gear and machining house in a chicken coop in Union Grove, WI, in 1947. This Company </strong><strong>is a division of the Regal Beloit Corporation.</strong></p>
<p>Grove Gear® drives are industry leaders in industrial-duty gear reducers and gear motors, and our worm speed reducers and ratio multipliers lead the power transmission industry in performance, cost savings, and reduced downtime. Grove Gear gear drives are found in crane, material handling, pulp and paper, and a host of additional industries where quality and dependability are paramount.</p>
<p>Grove Gear worm gear reducers are built with the best features available. From the one-piece cast housing to the high-strength steel alloy output shaft, each component is designed and manufactured to exacting standards for superior performance, quality and reliability.</p>
<p>Grove Gear Iron Man offers a comprehensive and superior line of gear reducers, available in right angle worm and high efficiency options.</p>
<p>Electra-Gear aluminium products are a lightweight, yet durable alternative to today’s cast iron and steel models, and are available off-the-shelf or customized to your exact specifications.</p>
<p>Bravo and Quadro reducers are lightweight, sealed for life, aluminium worm reducers designed for small spaces including OEM designs with difficult installations as well as mobile equipment.</p>
<p>LeCentric reducers are available as a compact aluminum or cast iron in-line reducer also sealed for life with ratings to 15HP (aluminium) 40HP (cast iron).</p>
