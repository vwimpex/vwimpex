<p>PMI LINEAR MOTION SYSTEM</p>
<p>PMI GROUP was established in 1990. They are involved in manufacturing linear rail, a critical component of precision machinery. The linear rail’s optimum design of geometric mechanics ensures the linear guide-way bears the load in all four directions, radial, reverse radial, and two lateral directions.</p>
<p>The rigidity of linear guideway can be easily achieved by preloading carriage and adding the number of carriages.</p>
<p>They also manufacture the Mono Stage KM series actuator, consisting of a linear guide-way and ball screw unit. PMI combine the carriage of linear guideway and nut of ball screw to a integral Carriage-Nut. The carriage-nut works with the U rail designed for high rigidity to achieve the high rigidity and high accuracy in the minimal space, especially to saving installation time.</p>
<p>The KM Actuator is mainly applied to Machine Tool, Electric Discharging Machine, Cutting Wire Machine, Plastics Injection Machine, Semi-conductor Equipment, Precision Orientation Equipment and other equipment and machines.</p>
