<p><strong>WM Berg</strong> has earned a reputation worldwide for quality, reliability and service. They offer precision machine parts in a variety of product lines with competitive pricing.</p>
<p>Bearings are highly engineered, precision-made components that enable machinery to move at extremely high speeds and carry remarkable loads with ease and efficiency. Berg offers many different options for your application, including custom bearings :</p>
<ul>
<li>Journal Bearings</li>
<li>Ball bearings</li>
<li>Linear Bearings</li>
<li>Needle Bearings</li>
<li>Roller & Thrust Bearings</li>
<li>Spherical Bearings</li>
<li>Oil-less Bearings</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Precision Bearing Materials & Uses</strong></p>
<p>Precision bearings from WM Berg are made using hardened steels as opposed to standard bearings which are typically made with low-carbon steel. Precision bearings are used in a wide range of applications in healthcare, aerospace, motion control and many other industries where greater accuracy, increased load capacity and superior system performance are required.</p>
