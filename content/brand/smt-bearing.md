<p>Formed in 1946, SMT- Nankai Seiko Co., Ltd. is a well-known manufacturer of stainless steel ball bearings in Japan.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Standard SMT Ball Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>SMT keeps a large amount of its regular bearings in its factory inventory. Such precision ball bearing series include SS6000, SS6200, SS6300, SS6800, SS6300, 5200, 6800, and 6900.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Stainless Steel Ball Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p> AISI 440C stainless steel ball bearings, resistant to rust, corrosion, and high temperature, available from stock, in the series of 6000, 6200, 6300, 6800, and 6900<br> AISI 304 stainless steel ball bearings, widely used as corrosion- and heat-resistant steel and also as nonmagnetic steel<br> AISI 630 stainless steel ball bearings, excellent in corrosion-resistance and able to be hardened by heat treatment</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Single Row Deep Groove Ball Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p> 6800/6900 Series - thin-walled ball bearings are suitable for compact design and reduction of weight in machinery and equipment.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Double Row Angular Contact Ball Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p> 5200 series double-row angular contact ball bearings, sustaining axial load in both directions.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Special SMT Ball Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>With its production system capable of high-mix and low-volume, SMT can manufacture high-precision special ball bearings of various materials (titanium, ceramics, etc.), shapes, lubricants, etc. SMT is an expert of specialty bearings, meeting a wide variety of the needs:</p>
<ol>
<li> Bearing for use in high temperature.</li>
<li> Bearings for use in acid or alkaline environments</li>
<li> Bearings for use as replacement parts for discontinued machinery and equipment</li>
<li> Bearings all-in-one with other parts into compact design</li>
<li> Special bearings no longer available due to small order quantity</li>
<li> Bearings for use in food processing machinery and equipment in compliance with HACCP (food hygiene management system)</li>
</ol>
<ul>
<li><strong>Titanium Ball Bearings (Titanium Rings with Ceramic Balls)</strong></li>
</ul>
<ol>
<li>SMT Titanium Ball Bearings</li>
<li>Full Ceramic Ball Bearings</li>
<li>Stainless Steel Ball Bearings with Aligning Ring</li>
</ol>
<p> SMT AISI 304 stainless steel ball bearings have much greater corrosion resistance than those made of SAE 52100 bearing steel or AISI 440C stainless steel.</p>
<p> Heat resistant grease is used in AISI 304 and AISI 440C stainless steel ball bearings.<br> SMT AISI 440C stainless steel bearings have a high resistance to corrosion and chemicals, and has high stability in high temperatures.</p>
