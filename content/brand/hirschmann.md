<p><strong>HIRSCHMANN's rod ends and spherical bearings </strong>are bearing elements which can be used for many applications, and are of the plain bearing type. They have proved their worth in millions of instances as a design element for transmitting power between members lying at various angles, both statically and dynamically. The mounting dimensions of these bearing elements have been fixed in DIN ISO 12240-4 (648 K). </p>
