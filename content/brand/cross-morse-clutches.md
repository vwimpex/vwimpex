<p>Cross+Morse is a Birmingham based manufacturer of power Transmission products and solutions with over 100 years of history (please visit our ‘Company History’ page for our complete timeline), right in the centre of the engineering hub in the West Midlands region in the United Kingdom.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Standard Roller Chains</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Precision Roller Chains to both British standard (BS) and American standard (ANSI) from 1/4" to 2.1/2" in simplex and multistrand forms. Suitable for drives with powers from fractional to 2500 kW, with operating speeds up to 25 metres per second.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Sprockets & Platewheels</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>An extensive range of sprockets and platewheels: simplex and multi strand version, pilot and taper bore, double simplex sprockets, finished bored and keyed and/or induction hardened.</p>
<ul>
<li>
<h2><strong>Special Construction & Attachment Chain</strong></h2>
</li>
</ul>
<p>We stock a large range of attachments for conveying and transportation, custom designs also available. Straight-side plate, hollow pin, extended pitch, metric pitch and special design chains on short lead-times.</p>
<ul>
<li>
<h2><strong>Stainless Steel Chain & Sprockets</strong></h2>
</li>
</ul>
<p>Standard BS and ANSI chain in 304 and 316 stainless steel, in simplex and multi-strand form. A comprehensive stock of SS sprockets completes this range. Corrosion resistant chains such as Aquaproof (AP) and electroless nickel plated are also available.</p>
<ul>
<li>
<h2><strong>Crossbore® Rework Service</strong></h2>
</li>
</ul>
<p>A dedicated production area with specialized CNC machines provides a rapid response rework service of standard sprockets, pulleys and gears. Known as Crossbore®; this service includes reboring, keywaying and setscrewing.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Chain Tools</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Roller Chain tools reduce time both in the field and in the shop and are a necessity for disconnecting roller chains from bulk length, or for chain repair and alteration purposes.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Torque Limiters and Couplings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>We offer the original series torque limiters and spares based on Imperial dimensions and a new range of metric torque limiters providing both smaller and larger units. Both series are available with a roller chain coupling.</p>
<ul>
<li>
<h2><strong>Sheargard Clutches and Couplings</strong></h2>
</li>
</ul>
<p>The Sheargard clutch uses shearpin wedges to provide total disengagement of driving and driven members in the event of an overload, it is suitable for high torques with a compact, low inertia unit. Available as a roller chain coupling and rubber elastic couplings.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Crossgard Overload Clutches and Couplings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Crossgard clutches and couplings all use sprung loaded balls locked in detents to provide drive and overload control. There are three types available: CG, CGX and CGZ all designed to provide the customer with a reliable, simple to operate clutch.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Sheargard Clutches and Couplings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>The Safegard CS series clutches and couplings were developed to provide overload protection for the transmission of high torques within a compact unit using balls or rollers. Available in four modes of drive operation: CSF, CSY, CSL and CSZ.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Safegard Type CZ/CN Overload Clutches and Couplings </strong></li>
</ul>
<p>The CZ/CN series of Overload clutches and couplings provides a totally backlash free transmission of power with a very responsive overload protection. Two basic modes of operation are available: Ratchetting and Synchron (F/Y), with option of expanding bush shaft connection and steel disc coupling.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Safegard Type CMZ Overload Clutches and Couplings </strong></li>
</ul>
<p>The CMZ Modular Rotation-Free series of Clutches and couplings have been designed for overload protection in high torque, high speed and high inertia drives; to disconnect quickly the driven machinery from the motor in the event of an overload allowing free rotation of the drive.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Industrial Ratchet Freewheels and Adaptors</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Cross+Morse offer IRF Clutches with external mounting flange and IRF sprockets to suit standard BS Series chains. We also have steel adaptors for industrial ratchet freewheel available.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>"M" Series Sprag Clutches</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Fully sealed, bearing supported, high torque Sprag Clutch Assemblies with Metric and Imperial Bores. 4 types of clutch are offered: MG, MI, MO and MR.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Backstop Types B200 & B500 </strong>Shaft mounted sprag clutch assemblies for backstop applications.</li>
</ul>
