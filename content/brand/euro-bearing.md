<p><strong>EURO-BEARING</strong> is UK based & 35 years in the industry, Euro-Bearings Ltd have considerable knowledge of Fork Truck Mast Bearings, Combined Roller Bearings, Linear Motion Systems, Tolerance Rings and many other related products</p>
<ul>
<li>COMBINED ROLLER BEARINGS & MATING STEEL PROFILES</li>
</ul>
<p>When used in conjunction with the corresponding steel channels, Combined Roller Bearings can be used to make a wide range of heavy duty linear motion systems. Applications can be horizontal, vertical, cantilevered or telescopic. The size of bearing should be selected using Hertzian Pressure</p>
<ul>
<li>STANDARD COMBINED ROLLER BEARINGS</li>
</ul>
<p>Originally used in fork trucks, combined roller bearings (or combi bearings / combined bearings) can be used in a variety of horizontal, vertical, cantilevered or telescopic linear motion applications.</p>
<ul>
<li>ECCENTRIC ADJUSTABLE COMBINED ROLLER BEARINGS</li>
</ul>
<p>Combined roller bearings are available from stock in standard, adjustable & Jumbo ranges. Each bearing has a corresponding steel section (channel) and is available with mounting plate for ease of assembly.</p>
<ul>
<li>SHIM ADJUSTABLE COMBINED ROLLER BEARINGS</li>
</ul>
<p>These combined bearings are adjustable by introducing shims between the hub and the axial roller carrier. This enables the overall height of the bearing to be altered.</p>
<ul>
<li>Radial ROLLER BEARINGS & Hub (no axial roller)</li>
</ul>
<p>These radial roller bearings can be used as an alternative to the standard combined roller bearing. Usually it is preferable to use the combined roller bearing as the side (axial) roller helps prevent crabbing, but in some situations it might be better to use a radial roller instead.</p>
<ul>
<li>VULKOLLAN COMBINED BEARINGS</li>
</ul>
<p>The main advantage of Vulkollan coated bearings is the reduction in noise. If you want a smooth, quiet yet robust linear motion system then this product is the solution. They are designed to run in the same mating steel profiles as our combined roller bearings and are fitted with an adjustable Oilamid rubbing block to provide axial guidance and help the system to run true.</p>
<ul>
<li>JUMBO COMBINED ROLLER BEARINGS</li>
</ul>
<p>The Jumbo combined bearings have been designed for use in heavy duty linear motion applications where it is not possible to use the Standard Combined Bearings because the contact force (Hertzian Pressure) is too high.</p>
<ul>
<li>PRECISION COMBINED ROLLER BEARINGS (PR4.0xx)</li>
</ul>
<p>These precision combined roller bearings have a larger outer diameter than the Standard Combined Roller Bearings & are designed to be used in conjunction with precision profiles.<br> The precision comes from the tighter clearance (0.2mm) between the bearing & channel.<br> If this level of precision is not required, then please look at our standard range.</p>
<p>Calculate which size you need using Hertzian Pressure.</p>
