<p>Siboni (Siboni S.p.a.) is an Italian company that designs and manufactures electric motors (brushed, brushless) and precision planetary gearboxes (and gearmotors).</p>
<p>The company was founded in 1957 by Enzo and Ivo Siboni, originally repairing small electric appliances and motors, later moving into motor manufacturing and gearbox (planetary) development.</p>
<p><strong>PRODUCT LINE:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>RE Series (High Torque)- The <strong>RE GEAR</strong> is designed to ensure a <strong>strong load transmission,</strong> precision and reliability. Six sizes are available in single or multi-stages thus offering a wide range of reduction ratios.</li>
<li>NXT Series (Precision / Dynamic) - Planetary gearboxes have a <strong>high torsional rigidity and good price/performance ratio</strong>. Five sizes are available in<strong> single or multi-stages</strong> thus offering a wide range of reduction ratios.</li>
<li>NXR Series - Gearboxes are characterised by their<strong> high torsional rigidity</strong> and <strong>strong load transmission</strong>.</li>
<li>NXS Series (Compact) - Designed to keep high performance in <strong>compact dimensions</strong>.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>TYPICAL APPLICATIONS</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Automation, robotics, and motion control systems</li>
<li>Machine tools, packaging machinery</li>
<li>Woodworking, textile, printing machinery</li>
<li>Healthcare, pharma‑equipment</li>
<li>Electric traction, conveyors, handling systems</li>
<li>Anywhere combination of motor + gearbox is needed for torque boost & speed reduction</li>
</ul>
