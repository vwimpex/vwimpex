<p><strong>KOYO BEARINGS</strong></p>
<p>JTEKT Corporation is a Japanese multi-national company having expertise in automotive steering systems, drivelines, machine tools, and automotive & industrial bearings.</p>
<p><strong>Koyo </strong>is one of the most recognized names in bearings, manufacturing products that are designed for virtually any application that relies on needle bearings. Under JTEKT they produce bearings that have a global reach.</p>
