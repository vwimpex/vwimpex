<p>Founded in 1981 in Italy, ISB Industries has become one of the major international players in the field of industrial components.</p>
<p>Today ISB is able to produce and distribute a complete range of high quality products, available on stock, to comply customers needings.</p>
<p>The internationally consolidated reputation and presence in over 90 countries in the world are born here: at the headquarter of ISB Industries. The new logistics center, the first one by extension in Italy and third one in Europe represents the technical and distribution core of the brand. The great availability of the products in the warehouse, allows us to offer customers one of the fastest pre and post sales services in the industry.</p>
