<p><strong>Martin</strong> since 1951, Martin Sprocket & Gear has been a leading manufacturer of power transmission products, material handling components and systems, and industrial hand tools. Expansion of their capabilities has led Martin Sprocket & Gear into the machining, fabricating, forging, casting, powdered metal technology and plastic injection molding of countless power transmission and conveying products.</p>
<p>Martin specializes in a wide variety of products, including:</p>
<ul>
<li> Bushings - All popular styles including QD, split taper, and tapered bore.</li>
<li> Couplings - Jaw, Quadraflex, MartinFlex (tire), and chain.</li>
<li> Sheaves/Pulleys - V-belt, synchronous (rectangular and rounded tooth).</li>
<li> Sprockets - A plate, B hub - plain and bored-to-size, C hub, engineered.</li>
<li> Gears - Spur, worm, miter, bevel, helical, rack.</li>
<li> Material handling - Screw conveyors, bucket elevators.</li>
<li> Industrial hand tools - Hammers, wrenches & sockets, spanners, picks.</li>
</ul>
<p>Martin has grown to include 29 manufacturing and sales offices throughout the United States and Mexico in addition to their headquarters in Arlington, Texas.</p>
<p>Their Livonia, Michigan facility has an impressive inventory which allows for same day availability of most power transmission components through Bearing Service, Inc.</p>
