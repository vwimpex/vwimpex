<p>In the 25 years since the company was founded, SWC gained global reputation and is highly appreciated by customers on every continent.</p>
<p>As a former part of FAG, the focus on quality aspects was allways one of the most important objectives.<br> Until today, SWC puts high emphasis on excellent quality products as well as individual consulting in order to ensure an additional value for our customers.</p>
<p>Since the beginning, the SWC Group highly values quality in every aspect, wether it is the quality of the manufactured products, the consulting service or the processes inside the family-run companies. Thereby one objective is paramount for us since more than 25 years:</p>
<p>SWC offers variouse products suitable for high temperature applications. In order to choose the right bearing, the temperature as well as the speeds and the load ratings have to be taken into account.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>High-temp Deep Groove Ball Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Coated bearings for extreme temperatures and slow speeds</strong></p>
<p><strong> HT1 (< 350°C) </strong> SWC High-temp Deep Groove Ball Bearings with the suffix HT1 are filled with a high-temp paste,<br> which hardens after reaching a certain temperature and leaves a powder.</p>
<p>Bearings lubricated with HT1 are suitable for slow speeds and temperatures up to 350°C,<br> but have to be relubricated or replaced after 12 to 18 months depending on the application.</p>
<p>All HT1 bearings are available open, with shield on one (ZR) or both sides (2ZR).</p>
<ul>
<li><strong> HT2 (< 280°C)</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>SWC High-temp Deep Groove Ball Bearings with the suffix HT2 are filled with a special grease.</p>
<p>Bearings lubricated with HT2 are suitable for slow speeds and temperatures up to 280°C.<br> Depending on the application, these bearings allow maintenance-free operation up to 5 years.</p>
<p>All HT2 bearings are available open, with shield on one (ZR) or both sides (2ZR).</p>
<ul>
<li><strong> HT2X (< 320°C)</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>SWC High-temp Deep Groove Ball Bearings with the suffix HT2X are filled with a special grease.</p>
<p>Bearings lubricated with HT2X are suitable for slow speeds and temperatures up to 320°C.<br> Depending on the application, these bearings allow maintenance-free operation up to 5 years.</p>
<p>All HT2X bearings are available open, with shield on one (ZR) or both sides (2ZR).</p>
<ul>
<li>
<strong> HT3 (< 300°C)</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>SWC High-temp Deep Groove Ball Bearings with the suffix HT3 are filled with a solid compound.<br> While running, small particals of the compound come lose and lubricate the bearing continously.</p>
<p>Bearings filled with HT3 are suitable for slow speeds and temperatures up to 300°C.<br> Depending on the application, these bearings allow maintenance-free operation up to 6 years.</p>
<p>All HT3 bearings are only available with shields on both sides (2ZR).</p>
