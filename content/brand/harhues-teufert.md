<p><strong>Linear Systems & Roller Bearings</strong></p>
<p>Innovative and reliable Since 1991, Harhues & Teufert has combined experience and expertise with technological advancement and entrepreneurial vision. The result is rolling bearing and linear technology products tailored to practical requirements, which are used in more than 20 countries around the world.</p>
<p>They supply all types of rolling bearings from our warehouse, including ball bearings, needle roller bearings, cylindrical roller bearings, pillow block bearings, flange bearings, as well as support rollers and cam followers, many sizes also available in rust-resistant or stainless-steel versions. Plastic plain bearings and plain bearings made of composite materials round out our product range.</p>
<p>Shaft guide systems consisting of support rails, ball bushings and bearing housings are also supplied.</p>
<p>This also includes their idlers of the HT28 series, old designation NE 41682.</p>
<p>They manufacture guide rails of the AFW or IFW series in a rust-resistant design, as well as the associated guide rollers of the FRWS, FRWSE, NFR or NFRE series.</p>
<p>Shaft guide systems consisting of support rails, ball bushings and bearing housings are also supplied from stock.</p>
<p>Technologically up-to-date products of high quality are their standard. The strict quality policy for the products they sell is crucial to the success of their company. With our quality management system according to DIN EN ISO 9001: 2015, they offer their customers excellent service and transparency in all processes.</p>
<p>The company <strong>HARHUES & TEUFERT GMBH</strong>, is a Manufacturer/ Producer, which operates in the Wheels, castors and rollers industry. It is based in Velbert, Germany.</p>
