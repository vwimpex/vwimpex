<p>New York based Schatz Bearing Corporation is a world-recognized manufacturing leader of the highest-quality precision bearings and linear motion products. Its products can be found in many applications, including the aerospace, defense, and automotive industries. The company is known for its innovation and adherence to industry-leading quality in bearing manufacturing. All of the products produced in their Poughkeepsie facility meet or exceed AS9100 Rev. C and ISO9001:2008 standards. The vast experience of the company has allowed them to design and produce specialized solutions for specific industry applications. The industries include Aerospace, Machine Tool, Automation and Robotics, Medical Equipment, Construction and Agricultural Machinery.</p>
<p>Whether your requirements are for low or high volumes, custom designs, or extremely clean bearing components, Schatz Bearing has the experience needed to meet the challenge.</p>

<ul>
<li>Airframe Control/Aerospace Bearings</li>
</ul>
<p>Airframe Control Bearings are the broadest category of products that NPB offers. This Ball Bearing product is really the go-to part for oscillation in the bearing world. Due to their versatility and variety, they are used for inserts for larger assemblies & pulleys, slight miss-alignment, or any application requiring slow rotation or oscillating motion. They are utilized across many non-aircraft industries due to their precision, radial and axial positioning, and excellence in oscillatory motion.<br> The most common industry standard for the airframe control bearings is the Society of Automotive Engineers (SAE) AS7949. This was formerly Mil-B-7949.</p>
<ul>
<li>Precision and Specialty Ball Bearings</li>
</ul>
<p>Schatz manufacturer's standard and custom designed ball bearings for a variety of industries. Their product features inch and metric series ball bearings available up to 14" in outside diameter. Ball bearings are available in 52100 Chrome steel or 440C Stainless and are available with numerous retainer and closure options.</p>
<ul>
<li>Precision Thin Section Bearings</li>
</ul>
<p>Thin section bearings are often specified where low torque is required and when weight and/or space are limited. In applications with these limitations, special lubricants and requirements for extreme cleanliness are common. Thin section bearings are deigned to have the same cross section with a range of bore sizes.</p>
