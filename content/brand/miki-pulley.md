<p><strong>Miki Pulley</strong>, established in 1939 at Japan, now a known Global Brand offering as High Speed , Zero backlash ,Servo Couplings, Electromagnetic Clutches and Brakes, Power Locks and several other products for Power Transmission and Control.</p>
<p><strong>Miki Pulley</strong> clutches, brakes and other speed control components are used by various industries for pumps, robotic arms, CNC machines, printing machines, packaging machines, stepper/servo motors, and much more. Learn more about industry applications for our motion control products by visiting the industry categories below or contact us to discuss your requirements.</p>
