<p><br> GPZ , Ukraine is the largest manufacturers of conical and cylindrical roller bearings with diameter up to 4500 mm. Besides the regularly used standard sizes, GPZ produces Non-Standard and technically unique bearings as per customers design and requirements.</p>
<p>The brand GPZ is registered with government of Ukraine and follows GOST ISO Standard and has proved and established itself in the bearing world through its quality and flexibility. As a major contributor in Steel Making, Oil and Gas, Power & Cement Industry, Large- Sizes bearings of GPZ are being used in rolling mills and in converter of metallurgical plants, drilling unit, cement kilns, Steel making shops and several other areas.</p>
<p>Developed industrial base and unique equipments allow GPZ to produce bearings with dimensions of 4-5 meters and weight of 11 tons with utmost precisions and quality.</p>
<p>GPZ has now entered into bigger network & establishment by servicing the Paper &, Sugar Industry/ Automobile & Automation industry.</p>

<p><br>
<strong> Products:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong> Deep Groove Ball Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>These bearing support higher loads than a shallower groove. They support both radial and axial loads.</p>
<ul>
<li>
<strong>Angular Contact Ball Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>These bearing are asymmetrical for manufacturing purpose. In angular contact the load is passes on the inner race is same as in the outer race. Due to this angular contact bearings better support combined loads ( both in axial and radial direction).</p>
<ul>
<li>
<strong>Cylindrical Roller Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>These roller bearings have higher radial load capacity and also suitable for high speed. If inner and outer races are misaligned , the bearing capacity often drop quickly.</p>
<ul>
<li>
<strong>Tapered Roller Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>These bearings uses conical roller that run on conical races. Tapered roller bearings support both radial and axial load and due to their greater contact area they carry higher loads.</p>
<ul>
<li>
<strong>Thrust Ball Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>These bearings used in low thrust application and they are designed to support axial load.</p>
<ul>
<li>
<strong> Spherical Roller Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>These bearings support a rotating shaft in the bore of the inner ring that may be misaligned in respect to the outer ring. These ring allows rotation with low friction.</p>
<ul>
<li>
<strong>Needle Roller Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>These bearings uses long, thin cylindrical rollers resembling needles, they are used to reduce the friction of a rotating surface. Due to greater surface area they can support greater load.</p>
<ul>
<li>
<strong>Self-Aligning Ball Bearings</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>These bearing was used mainly in bearing arrangements with very long shafts, such as transmission shafts in textile factories.</p>
