<p>DURBAL formerly <strong>Durbal Schlegel</strong>, is a manufacturer of heavy-duty plain bearing and anti-friction bearing rods ends. The company was originally founded in 1938 in Germany by Wolfgang Schlegel, an amateur pilot.</p>
<p>Durbal offers three lines of rod ends: Premium Line Rod Ends, Classic Line Rod Ends, and Basic Line Rod Ends.</p>
<p>Durbal Premium Line Rod Ends are low maintenance, lubrication possible rod ends with integral self-aligning roller or ball bearing.</p>
