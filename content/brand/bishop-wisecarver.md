<p>The Bishop-Wisecarver Group is a WBENC certified woman-owned family of companies who work with manufacturers to engineer, manufacture, and build custom complex assemblies, linear motion solutions and optimal embedded intelligence systems. Through the integration of mechanical, electrical, software, control and systems design engineering expertise, 65+ years of experience and 20, 000+ unique clients, They continue to provide a single point of service that results in custom designs, increased efficiencies, and accelerated time to market.<br> WC, now in its second generation, has remained one of the most respected names in automation solutions and guided motion technology since 1950. Bishop Wisecarver products include the Dualvee guide wheel system, LoPro linear actuator, Quicktrack linear guides, Madewell guide wheels, Utilitrak compact linear guides, and Minivee guide systems.</p>
